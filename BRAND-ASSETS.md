# Manufacturer brand assets

These marks are used only to identify the engine, gearbox or manufacturer
covered by an editorial guide. They do not imply endorsement, dealership or
affiliation. The site owner confirmed authorisation to use manufacturer marks
on 11 September 2026.

## Local vector sources

- Volvo Penta, Yanmar, Cummins, Perkins, Kubota, ZF, Isuzu, Doosan and Kohler:
  vector logo files obtained from Wikimedia Commons file pages.
- Caterpillar, John Deere, Iveco, Scania, MAN and Hyundai: Simple Icons vector
  marks, with the manufacturer colour supplied by that collection.

The imported SVGs are self-hosted, contain no scripts or external references,
and total less than 60 KB. Specialist, legacy and multi-brand guides use a
typographic identifier instead of presenting one current company logo as the
identity for the whole subject.

All trademarks and brand marks remain the property of their respective owners.
