# Template — Location × Service
`/services/{service}/{location}/` · 900–1,400 words
Gate: ≥5 verified operators offering this service (≥3 tier 2)

## H1
{Service} in {Location}

## Sections
1. **Opening (100w)** — The local angle. Why demand exists here: fleet mix,
   water conditions, seasonality, berth types.
2. **When you need it (200w)** — Symptoms leading here; link the matching
   `/marine-engine-problems/{symptom}/` pages.
3. **What the job involves (250w)** — The real process, how long, and what a good
   operator does differently. Include whether it is dockside, workshop or
   haul-out work — this is the marine-specific detail that matters most.
4. **What it costs here (150w + table)** — Local band, parts/labour split, the
   access assumption stated, dated.
5. **Operators offering it here** — Filtered subset of the location list.
   Disclosure above the block.
6. **What to ask before booking (150w)** — Service-specific.
7. **FAQ (4–6 Q)**

## Do not
Reuse the national service page with the location name swapped in. If there is no
genuine local angle, the page fails its gate — fold it into the location hub.

## Internal links
Up: `/marine-mechanics/{location}/` and `/services/{service}/` ·
Across: symptom pages, cost page

`BreadcrumbList` + `ItemList` of `LocalBusiness` + `FAQPage`
