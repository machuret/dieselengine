# Template — Guide
`/guides/{topic}/` · 1,200–2,500 words depending on cluster

Four clusters, four postures:

**cost** — Follow `templates/cost.md`. Lead with the number.

**comparison** — Give a real recommendation, not a balanced non-answer.
Structure: the short answer → decision table across the axes that actually
matter → when each option wins → what most people should do. A comparison page
that refuses to pick a side ranks and converts badly.

**buyers-guide** — Actionable checklist the reader can take to a workshop or
aboard. Printable/saveable. Ends in a referral to the relevant location hub.

**compliance** — Sourced and cited to the actual instrument (the AMSA-administered
national system for domestic commercial vessels, the state recreational
authority, Australian Consumer Law), with the date checked. Written as
orientation, never as compliance advice, and never as a workaround. Reviewed
before publish and marked `compliance_review: required` — see CONTENT-PLAN.md §9.

**education** — Explain the mechanism, not just the term. Diagrams earn their
place here, particularly for cooling circuits, saildrives and gearboxes. These are the pages that attract links, so make them the ones a
mechanic would send to a customer.

## Every guide
- Named author and, for technical content, a named licensed marine mechanic reviewer
- `Article` + `FAQPage` schema
- Links up to its pillar and down to the specific service, symptom or engine
  pages it references
