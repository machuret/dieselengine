# Template — Cost
`/costs/{service}/` and `/costs/{service}/{location}/` · 900–1,400 words
Gate (location variant): ≥5 real local quotes, else fold into the national page

Highest-converting intent on the site. Also the highest credibility risk — wrong
numbers destroy trust permanently.

## H1
{Service} Cost in {Australia|Location} ({Year})

## Sections
1. **The short answer (80w + range, above the fold)** — Give the number
   immediately. Readers who have to scroll for it leave.
2. **Price table** — Low / typical / high, split into parts and labour, with the
   vessel class each band applies to. **State the access assumption** — dockside,
   mooring by tender, or haul-out. Access is the dominant price variable in
   marine work and a range without it is meaningless.
3. **What drives the price up or down (250w)** — Engine type, parts availability,
   dealer vs independent, whether related work is needed at the same time.
4. **Regional variation (150w)** — National page: how locations differ. Location
   page: why this one sits where it does (labour rates, parts freight, berth
   types, seasonality, slipway availability).
5. **What's usually included — and what isn't (150w)** — The extras that turn a
   quote into a bigger bill.
6. **How to compare quotes (200w)** — What a complete quote contains; what a
   suspiciously cheap quote usually leaves out.
7. **Your rights (100w)** — Quote vs estimate, authorisation for extra work,
   and repair warranty under Australian Consumer Law.
8. **FAQ (4–6 Q)**

## Non-negotiables
- Every figure dated: "Prices current as at {Month Year}."
- Haul-out quoted separately — it is a different supplier and a fixed cost
- State the basis: number of quotes, cities sampled.
- Re-check every 6 months; update the date or remove the page.
- **No `Offer` schema.** We do not sell this service.
