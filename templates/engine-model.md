# Template — Engine Model
`/engine-brands/{brand}/{engine-family}/` · 1,500–2,500 words
Gate: engine-specific fault data, mechanic-reviewed

This is the highest-value page type on the site: lowest competition, most
durable, most linkable. Treat it accordingly.

## H1
{Brand} {Engine} Engine: Problems, Specs and Repair

## Sections
1. **What it is (150w)** — Production years, what it replaced, what replaced it.
2. **Specifications (table)** — Displacement, configuration, bore/stroke,
   compression ratio, injection system, aspiration, power/torque, emissions
   standard. **Every figure verified against manufacturer documentation.**
3. **Australian fitment (table)** — Every AU-delivered vehicle and model year
   this engine was fitted to. This table alone earns links.
4. **Known problems (the core, 600–900w)** — Each fault as its own H3:
   symptom → cause → typical km band it appears at → diagnosis →
   repair → cost range. Rank by how commonly it actually occurs.
5. **Service intervals and known-good maintenance (200w)** — Oil spec, filter
   intervals, timing chain/belt intervals, and what shortens this engine's life.
6. **Rebuild vs replace (250w)** — Rebuild cost band in Australia, exchange
   engine availability, when a swap makes more sense.
7. **Finding a specialist** — Link to `/engine-brands/{brand}/specialists/{city}/`
   pages where they exist, else the brand hub.
8. **FAQ (6–8 Q)**

## Internal links
Up: brand hub · Across: 2–3 symptom pages for its characteristic faults ·
Out: rebuild cost page, engine swap service page

## Schema
`TechArticle` + `FAQPage` + `BreadcrumbList`, with `author` and `reviewedBy`

## Byline
Named author + named licensed mechanic reviewer + review date. Mandatory.
