# Marine diesel at Rozelle Bay

The working end of Sydney Harbour. Rozelle Bay and the surrounding Blackwattle
foreshore hold the commercial capability, the hardstand and the larger-vessel
engineering that the rest of the harbour lacks.

## What it is for

Sydney's engine trade is overwhelmingly mobile, because the fleet is
overwhelmingly moored. Rozelle Bay is the counterweight: the place for work that
cannot be done from a tender.

- **Engine removal and repower**, where the boat has to come out or open up
- **Commercial and charter vessels**, with the survey obligations attached
- **Larger recreational craft** that most harbour facilities cannot lift
- **Shaft, gearbox and running gear** work needing the boat hauled

For a Sydney owner the practical point is that hardstand across the basin is the
genuinely scarce resource, and this is one of the few places that has it near
the city.

## Working water

This is a commercial harbour environment with ferry traffic, workboats and
industrial foreshore activity. Two consequences:

**Stray current is worth considering.** Shore power infrastructure in a working
precinct raises the possibility of corrosion driven by an electrical fault
rather than by ordinary galvanic action — and that version is fast enough to
consume a propeller in a season. A change in anode wastage rate is the signal to
investigate rather than to fit more.

**The water carries more than the outer harbour does.** Runoff from a heavily
urbanised catchment ends up here, and raw water strainers show it.

## Commercial standards

Workshops serving survey vessels work to documentation and scheduling standards
that private owners can benefit from simply by asking. A maintenance record kept
to commercial expectations costs nothing extra and is worth real money when you
sell.

The corresponding caution is the same one that applies in any commercially
oriented precinct: confirm a recreational job is **quoted as a recreational
installation** rather than to commercial scale.

## Booking

Hardstand near the city is contested and expensive. Book it first; the workshop
diary fits around the lift, not the other way round.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is Rozelle Bay for?**
Work that cannot be done from a tender — repowers, hauls, running gear, and
commercial or larger recreational vessels.

**Why is hardstand so hard to get in Sydney?**
Capacity across the basin is limited and contested, which is why the lift date
usually determines when a job happens.

**Should I worry about stray current here?**
It is worth watching. A working harbour with heavy shore power infrastructure
raises the possibility, and a change in anode wastage rate is the signal.

**Anything to check when getting recreational work done?**
Confirm the job is quoted as a recreational installation rather than to
commercial scale.

**What should I book first?**
The haul-out. Everything else fits around it.
