# Marine diesel at Manly

Moreton Bay's main boat harbour, and the largest concentration of recreational
marine service capability in Brisbane.

## Bay water, and what it does

Moreton Bay is shallow, sandy and across much of it turbid. Boats here draw sand
and suspended sediment through the raw water circuit continuously.

The effects are cumulative rather than dramatic, which is exactly why they get
missed:

**Strainers fill faster than owners expect.** Not a fault — a local condition.

**Heat exchangers silt from the inside.** Nothing warns you. The engine runs a
degree or two warmer each season, and then one hot afternoon under load it does
not cope.

This is the reason the classic Brisbane complaint is an overheat that survives
an impeller replacement. The impeller was not the problem; it was just the
accessible part.

**Anodes go briskly** in warm water, including the pencil anode inside the heat
exchanger that most owners have never seen.

## Berthed, not moored — which helps

Unlike Sydney's mooring-heavy fleet, Manly is marina berthing. A mechanic drives
to the boat and works alongside, which makes mobile servicing genuinely
straightforward and removes the weather dependency that complicates tender-based
work elsewhere.

The constraint here is **haul-out**, not access. Anything below the waterline
needs the yard booked, and that is the resource to secure first.

## Choosing intervals for the bay

The most useful local adjustment is simple and rarely made: **service the raw
water side on a bay schedule, not a manual schedule.** Strainer checks more
often, heat exchanger cleaning on a shorter cycle, and the pencil anode actually
inspected.

That one change prevents most of what walks into Brisbane workshops as an
overheating complaint.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why does my engine overheat in Moreton Bay?**
Shallow, sandy and often turbid water loads the raw water circuit. Silted heat
exchangers and clogged strainers are far more common than engine faults.

**I replaced the impeller and it still overheats. What now?**
The heat exchanger. The impeller is the accessible part, not usually the cause
in this water.

**Is mobile servicing practical at Manly?**
Yes — it is marina berthing, so a mechanic works alongside. That is easier than
tender-based work elsewhere.

**What should I book first for a below-waterline job?**
The haul-out. Access to the boat is easy here; yard capacity is the constraint.

**What is the most useful local habit?**
Servicing the raw water side on a bay schedule rather than a generic manual
interval.
