# Marine diesel at Rivergate

Rivergate is the heavy end of Brisbane's marine trade. It exists for the jobs
that a marina berth cannot accommodate: engines out, boats out of the water, and
work measured in weeks rather than hours.

## What it is for

Most engine servicing in Brisbane happens at the berth. Rivergate is where you
go when that is not possible:

- **Repowers**, where the engine has to come out through the deck or a hatch
- **Rebuilds**, where components go away and come back
- **Shaft, gearbox and running gear work** needing the boat hauled
- **Refit**, where the engine job is part of something larger

The hardstand and lift capability is the point. In a city where most work is
mobile, this is the facility that makes the difference between a job being
practical and being deferred indefinitely.

## The river is hard on raw water circuits

The Brisbane River runs muddy. After heavy rain it runs very muddy, and it
carries debris with it.

A boat berthed up the river draws a markedly dirtier raw water supply than one
in Moreton Bay, and the consequences are cumulative rather than dramatic:
strainers filling, heat exchangers silting from the inside, and an engine
running a few degrees warmer each season until something gives.

**If your boat lives on the river, shorten the raw water intervals.** That is
the single most useful local adjustment, and it is the one most often skipped
because nothing warns you.

## Flood history is a fair question

The river floods, and boats and facilities along it have been affected more than
once. It shapes insurance, where owners keep boats through the wet season, and
what a second-hand engine sourced locally may have been through.

If you are buying a boat or an engine from along this river, ask. It is a
reasonable question and a well-understood one locally.

## Tide and access

River facilities work to tides. Haul-out and relaunch windows frame a job's
programme, so ask for those dates first — the workshop diary fits around them
rather than the other way round.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is Rivergate for?**
Work that cannot be done at a berth: repowers, rebuilds, running gear and refit,
where the boat or the engine has to come out.

**Does river berthing affect my engine?**
Yes. The Brisbane River carries silt and debris, especially after rain, so raw
water circuits foul faster than in the bay.

**How much should I shorten service intervals?**
Enough that the heat exchanger and strainer are checked well before symptoms
appear. A river-berthed boat should not be on a generic manual interval.

**Is flood history worth asking about?**
Yes, for any boat or engine sourced along the river. It is a normal question
here.

**What sets the timeline for a haul-out job?**
Tide-dependent access. Get the haul-out and relaunch dates first; everything
else fits between them.
