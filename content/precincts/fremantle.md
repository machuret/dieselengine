# Marine diesel at Fremantle

Fremantle is the industrial end of Western Australia's marine trade. The
recreational fleet north at Hillarys and south at Mandurah is larger, but the
engineering that keeps serious vessels running is here.

## Commercial capability, available to private owners

The fishing fleet, the offshore support industry and the port between them
sustain capability that a recreational market this size would not: machining,
fabrication, injection service, component rebuild and slipways sized for working
vessels.

For a private owner that means the hard jobs do not have to leave the state.
A rebuild that would otherwise wait on components travelling east can be done
here, and that is worth more in Western Australia than anywhere else in the
country.

The corresponding caution is scope. If your normal customers are commercial,
your quotes tend to be too. Confirm the job is **priced as a recreational
installation** rather than to commercial scale.

## Straight into the Indian Ocean

A boat leaving Fremantle is in open ocean immediately. There is no sheltered
run-out, no gradual transition, and the afternoon sea breeze builds a short,
steep chop through summer with complete reliability.

The engine consequence is specific and common: **a tank that behaves on the way
out finds its sludge on the way home.** Motion stirs settled water and debris
into the pickup, and filters block in the worst conditions of the day.

A primary water-separating filter with a vacuum gauge is not a refinement here.
It lets you see restriction building before it becomes a stall.

The second consequence is power. An engine chosen at the bottom of its design
range is working hardest exactly when the sea breeze is up.

## The parts question

Everything true of Perth is true here. Anything not held in Western Australia
comes across the Nullarbor by road or air, and both cost days.

Fremantle's advantage is that the commercial trade holds real stock, because its
customers cannot wait either. Ask what is on the shelf before authorising work.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Fremantle better for major work than Hillarys or Mandurah?**
For heavy engineering, machining and rebuild work, generally yes — the
commercial fleet sustains capability the recreational marinas do not.

**Anything to watch when getting recreational work done?**
Confirm the job is quoted as a recreational installation rather than to
commercial scale.

**Why does my engine struggle coming home?**
The afternoon sea breeze stirs the fuel tank. Settled water and sludge reach the
pickup and the filters block.

**What should I fit for Indian Ocean running?**
A primary water-separating filter with a vacuum gauge, and adequate power
margin. There is no sheltered water to limp home through.

**Are parts easier here?**
The commercial trade holds real stock because its customers cannot wait. Ask
what is on the shelf rather than what can be ordered.
