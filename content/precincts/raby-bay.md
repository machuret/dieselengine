# Marine diesel at Raby Bay

A canal estate marina at Cleveland, on the shallow southern side of Moreton Bay,
with Stradbroke and the southern bay islands directly across the water.

## Shallow water, everywhere

Southern Moreton Bay is skinny. Depths across the crossing to Stradbroke and
through the island channels are modest, tides matter, and boats regularly work
in water where the intake is not far off the bottom.

That produces the defining local engine issue: **sand and sediment in the raw
water circuit**, continuously rather than occasionally.

- Strainers fill faster than owners expect
- Impellers abrade and lose efficiency before they fail outright
- Heat exchangers silt from the inside, with no warning until an overheat

The local adjustment is straightforward: shorten the raw water intervals. Check
the strainer more often than feels necessary, clean the heat exchanger before
symptoms rather than after, and actually inspect the pencil anode inside it.

## Canal berths and short runs

The estate is a canal system, and the characteristic use pattern is a slow run
out through the canals, a crossing, and a slow run back.

Prolonged light loading glazes bores and builds carbon, and a boat used this way
for years can be in worse internal condition than one with far more hours worked
properly. Running the engine up under genuine load regularly is the mitigation,
and it has to be deliberate.

## Groundings happen here

Shallow water and sandbanks mean touching the bottom is a normal part of local
boating rather than a disaster. What is not normal is ignoring it.

After any grounding, check the propeller, the shaft, the cutless bearing and the
alignment. Alignment damage in particular shows up months later as vibration or
a stern gland that will not settle — and the gearbox eventually pays for it.

## Attendance

Canal berthing means mobile servicing is straightforward. Slipping and hardstand
are at Manly or the river precincts; book the lift first.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why do my strainers fill so fast at Raby Bay?**
Southern Moreton Bay is shallow and sandy, so intakes draw sediment
continuously. It is a local condition, not a fault.

**I replaced the impeller and it still overheats.**
The heat exchanger. In this water it silts from the inside and nothing warns you
until load exposes it.

**Is short canal running bad for the engine?**
Prolonged light loading glazes bores and builds carbon. Run it up under real
load regularly.

**I touched a sandbank. What should I check?**
Propeller, shaft, cutless bearing and alignment. Alignment damage often appears
months later as vibration.

**Where do I get the boat hauled?**
Manly or the river precincts. Book the lift before the workshop.
