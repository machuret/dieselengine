# Marine diesel at Sanctuary Cove

A marina village on the Coomera River with berths for large vessels, and an
owner profile that changes how engine work gets done here rather than what
needs doing.

## Large boats, integrated systems

The berths here hold a higher proportion of large motor yachts than most
Australian marinas, and those boats are not simply bigger versions of smaller
ones. They carry integrated systems — electronic engine management, proprietary
display and control networks, stabilisers, hydraulics, generators — and the
engine is one node in that.

The practical consequence: **diagnosing an engine fault sometimes means
diagnosing the network around it.** A technician with the right manufacturer
tooling is worth considerably more here than on a mechanically simple boat, and
it is the first thing to confirm before booking.

Twin installations are the norm, which means servicing is quoted per engine and
the annual figure surprises owners who have moved up from a single.

## The generator nobody schedules

On a boat with air conditioning and full accommodation, the generator frequently
accumulates more hours than either main engine, and it has its own raw water
circuit, impeller, anodes and service schedule.

It is the most reliably neglected diesel on this class of boat, and it is the
one whose failure ends a weekend.

## The water

The Coomera River is tidal, shallow in sections and carries silt from upstream —
more after heavy rain. Boats berthed here draw a dirtier raw water supply than a
Broadwater-moored boat, which means strainers and heat exchangers on a shorter
cycle than a generic manual interval.

**Draft matters on the approach.** If your boat is deep, confirm access and the
tide window rather than assuming.

## Everything is close

Coomera's engineering is minutes away, which is the real advantage of berthing
here. Specialist work — injection, machining, turbo, gearbox — does not require
moving the boat far, and comparison quotes are genuinely easy to obtain.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do large motor yachts need a different kind of mechanic?**
Often yes. Electronic engine management and integrated control networks mean
manufacturer diagnostic tooling matters more than on a simple boat.

**Why is my annual servicing bill so high?**
Twin engines, a generator, and often stabilisers and hydraulics on the same
schedule. Servicing is quoted per engine.

**What gets neglected most on these boats?**
The generator. It frequently runs more hours than the mains and has its own raw
water circuit, impeller and anodes.

**Does river berthing affect servicing intervals?**
Yes. The Coomera carries silt, particularly after rain, so the raw water side
needs attention on a shorter cycle.

**Is there a draft limit?**
Sections of the river are shallow and tidal. Confirm access and the tide window
if your boat is deep.
