# Marine diesel at Runaway Bay

A Broadwater marina precinct with a working service trade attached — the place
much of the Gold Coast's routine engine work actually happens, as distinct from
the heavy work up the Coomera River.

## Broadwater water, not river water

This matters more than owners expect. A boat berthed here draws from the
Broadwater rather than from the Coomera, and the two are different supplies.

**The Broadwater is warm, shallow and in places silty**, but it is cleaner than
the river and it does not carry the post-rain sediment load that the Coomera
does. Raw water circuits here have an easier time than a river-berthed boat's —
though still a harder time than a temperate-water boat's.

The local adjustment remains the same in kind, just less severe: strainer checks
and heat exchanger cleaning on a shorter cycle than a generic interval, and the
pencil anode inside the heat exchanger actually inspected.

## Short-run canal use is the quiet problem

The characteristic Gold Coast pattern is a boat that runs twenty minutes at
displacement speed through the canals to the Broadwater, anchors, and runs
twenty minutes back.

That is prolonged light loading, and it is exactly what glazes bores and builds
carbon. An engine used this way for years can be in worse condition than one
with three times the hours worked properly.

**The mitigation is deliberate**: run the engine up under real load regularly
rather than only idling to the sandbank and back. It costs a little fuel and it
is the difference between an engine reaching its design life and not.

## Attendance

Marina berthing means mobile servicing is straightforward — a mechanic works
alongside, without the weather dependency that complicates tender-based work in
mooring-heavy ports.

For anything below the waterline, the Coomera facilities are minutes away, which
is the structural advantage of being on this coast. Book the lift first.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Broadwater water easier on an engine than the river?**
Somewhat. It is cleaner and does not carry the Coomera's post-rain sediment
load, though it is still warm and shallow.

**Is short canal running bad for my engine?**
Prolonged light loading glazes bores and builds carbon. Run the engine up under
real load regularly rather than only idling to the Broadwater and back.

**Is mobile servicing practical here?**
Yes — marina berthing means a mechanic works alongside without weather
dependency.

**Where does the heavy work happen?**
The Coomera precinct, minutes away. Book the lift before the workshop.

**What is most often skipped?**
The pencil anode inside the heat exchanger, and heat exchanger cleaning
generally.
