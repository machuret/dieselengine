# Diesel Injector Replacement in Australia

Injectors are the highest-volume expensive repair on modern Australian diesels.
A common rail injector opens and closes several times per combustion cycle at up
to 2,500 bar, metering fuel quantities measured in cubic millimetres. It is a
precision instrument operating in a hostile environment, and it wears.

## Symptoms of failing injectors

- Rough idle, particularly when cold, sometimes with a distinct knock
- Black smoke under load; white smoke on a badly leaking injector
- Hard starting or extended cranking
- Loss of power, poor economy
- Diesel in the engine oil, from an injector leaking into the cylinder
- Cylinder-specific misfire codes or high injector correction values

## Diagnosis before replacement

This matters more here than almost anywhere else on the site, because injectors
are expensive and are frequently replaced when they were not the fault.

**Leak-off test.** Measures return flow from each injector. An injector
returning far more than the others is passing internally. Cheap, quick, and
genuinely diagnostic.
→ [Failed injector leak-off test](/diesel-problems/diesel-injector-leak-off/)

**Live correction values.** The ECU trims fuelling per cylinder to keep the
engine smooth. Large positive or negative corrections on one cylinder point at
that injector — or at low compression on that cylinder, which is a different and
much bigger problem.

**Bench testing.** The definitive answer: the injector on a calibrated test
bench, measuring delivery and spray pattern against specification.
→ [Diesel injector testing](/services/diesel-injector-testing/)

**Compression test.** Because a "dead cylinder" can be a mechanical problem
wearing an injector's clothing. A workshop that replaces an injector on a
cylinder with low compression has wasted your money.

## Replace all, or just the faulty one?

Usually all of them on the affected bank, and here is why: injectors wear at
similar rates, so one failing at 180,000km means the others are close behind.
The labour to access them is the same whether you do one or four, and doing one
means paying that labour again shortly.

The counter-argument is real on high-cost injectors and on engines where access
is easy — if only one has failed early and the others test within specification,
replacing one is defensible. Ask for the test results on all of them before
deciding.

## Reconditioned versus new

Properly remanufactured injectors — stripped, worn parts replaced, recalibrated
on a bench, with a test certificate — are a legitimate option and considerably
cheaper than new. Ask for the test data.

Avoid injectors sold as "cleaned" or "tested" with no calibration certificate.
An uncalibrated injector fitted to a modern common rail engine will not run
correctly and can damage the engine.

## Coding and adaptation

Modern injectors carry a calibration code that must be programmed into the ECU
when fitted. Skipping this step is one of the most common causes of a vehicle
that runs worse after an injector job than before it. Confirm the workshop can
code your vehicle's injectors before you book.

## Why they failed

Injectors rarely fail for no reason. Fuel contamination — water or particulate —
is the leading cause in Australia, especially in regional and remote areas.
Fitting new injectors without addressing contaminated fuel, a failed filter, or a
degrading high-pressure pump means doing the job twice.

## Frequently asked questions

**How long should diesel injectors last?**
On clean fuel and correct servicing, commonly 150,000–250,000km, though it
varies widely by engine and duty cycle. Contaminated fuel shortens this
dramatically.

**Can injectors be cleaned instead of replaced?**
Ultrasonic cleaning can help a lightly fouled injector, but a worn nozzle or a
failed internal seal cannot be cleaned back to specification. Insist on bench
test results either way.

**Do I have to replace all the injectors at once?**
Not necessarily, but usually it is the better economic decision because of
shared labour and similar wear. Get all of them tested before deciding.

**Why does my engine run worse after new injectors?**
Most commonly the calibration codes were not programmed into the ECU, or the
injectors were not correctly matched to the engine.

**Will new injectors fix my DPF problem?**
If a leaking injector was over-fuelling and blocking the filter, fixing it stops
the cause — but the filter still needs cleaning separately.
