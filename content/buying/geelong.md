# Buy a marine diesel engine in Geelong

Geelong is Melbourne's western boating market with an industrial engineering
base of its own, and for major work that combination is often better value than
crossing the bay.

## What the local market looks like

Corio Bay carries the fleet — Royal Geelong and the waterfront marinas, St
Helens, and the Bellarine harbours at Portarlington and Queenscliff. The
service trade is proportionate, and behind it sits Geelong's long industrial
engineering tradition: machining, fabrication and diesel work at a real scale.

Melbourne is an hour away, which means:

- **Dealer breadth is effectively Melbourne's**
- **Local yard and hardstand time is easier to get and generally cheaper**
- **Machining for a rebuild can often be done locally**

That makes Geelong a sensible place to have major work done even for boats
normally kept elsewhere on Port Phillip.

## What local conditions do to the choice

**Corio Bay is shallow and, in its inner reaches, silty.** Raw water intakes
pick up more than they do in the deeper parts of Port Phillip, so strainer and
heat exchanger access is worth specifying.

**Port Phillip is cold and has a real season.** As in Melbourne, the dominant
maintenance risk for a recreational boat is long layup rather than hard use.
Buying a low-hour boat here needs the same scepticism — read
[why low hours is not good news](/guides/low-hours-is-not-good-news/).

**The Heads.** Boats out of Queenscliff and Portarlington are close to Port
Phillip Heads, which is one of the more demanding entrances in the country.
Adequate power margin matters for anything that goes outside.

**Rip Bank and the Bellarine fishing grounds** put boats in exposed water
quickly from the southern harbours.

**Winter is the buying season.** Yard capacity, workshop diaries and quoting
are all better in the cooler months, and the boat loses no season.

## What to check before you commit

- Whether local machining can support a rebuild
- Yard and hardstand availability, which is a Geelong advantage — use it
- Strainer and heat exchanger access for inner Corio Bay berths
- Power margin if the boat goes outside the Heads
- Melbourne comparison quotes on the engine itself

## Frequently asked questions

**Where do I buy a marine diesel engine in Geelong?**
Local marine service around Corio Bay and the Bellarine, with Melbourne an hour
away for dealer breadth and Geelong's own industrial engineering behind it.

**Is Geelong cheaper than Melbourne for a repower?**
Yard and hardstand time is generally easier to get and less expensive. Compare
the whole job rather than the engine.

**Does Corio Bay water affect servicing?**
The inner bay is shallow and silty. Strainers and heat exchangers foul faster
than in the deeper parts of Port Phillip.

**When should I book work?**
Winter. The fleet is laid up, yards have capacity, and you lose no season.

**What power margin do I need for the Heads?**
More than a bay-only boat needs. Port Phillip Heads is unforgiving of an engine
chosen at the bottom of its design range.
