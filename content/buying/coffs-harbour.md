# Buy a marine diesel engine in Coffs Harbour

Coffs sits almost exactly halfway between Sydney and Brisbane, which is the
single most useful fact about buying an engine here.

## What the local market looks like

The Coffs Harbour International Marina and the harbour precinct hold the local
capability, serving a commercial fishing fleet, a charter and dive operation,
and a recreational fleet that fishes offshore.

It is a genuinely regional market. There is competent local marine service, and
dealer breadth is limited. For a significant purchase, both capitals are within
about half a day's drive, and comparison quotes from each are realistic.

The harbour is also a well-used stop for boats moving up and down the coast,
which keeps the local trade familiar with transient work and departure
deadlines.

## Midway is leverage

Being equidistant from two large markets is worth using deliberately:

- Get quotes from Sydney and Brisbane as well as locally
- Weigh the delivery cost of moving the boat against the price difference
- Ask a local workshop what they can source and how quickly, since they buy
  from both directions

The practical constraint is not availability but who will support the engine
afterwards. Weight that heavily.

## What local conditions do to the choice

**The harbour is small and the coast is open.** Boats are offshore within
minutes of leaving, and the fleet fishes well out. This is a coast with real
swell and it deserves an engine with margin.

**The East Australian Current** runs strongly down this section of coast. Boats
work against or with it, and it affects passage planning and fuel burn more than
owners expect further south.

**Warm, clean water.** Cooling conditions are moderate — this is sub-tropical
water without the intensity of the Far North. Anode consumption is brisk but not
extreme.

**Whale and game seasons** bring peak activity and tighter workshop capacity.

**Limited hardstand.** Yard and slipway capacity in a regional harbour is
finite. Book well ahead, or plan to take the boat to a capital for major work.

## What to check before you commit

- Who supports the engine family locally after the sale
- Comparison quotes from both Sydney and Brisbane
- Hardstand and slipway availability, booked early
- Whether the quote covers exhaust, propeller and instruments
- Power margin appropriate to offshore work on an open coast
