# Buy a marine diesel engine in Mackay

Mackay's marine market runs on commercial fishing, workboats and a recreational
fleet that has to deal with one of the largest tidal ranges on the Australian
east coast.

## What the local market looks like

The Mackay Marina and harbour precinct is the centre, with slipway and hardstand
capability and workshops oriented towards a hard-working fleet. The mining and
sugar industries behind the city sustain heavy diesel engineering that the marine
trade draws on.

It sits between Gladstone and the Whitsundays, both a couple of hours away,
which gives owners genuine options for comparison and for specialist work.

Characteristics of the market:

- **Commercial-oriented workshops** with real engineering discipline
- **Limited recreational dealer breadth**, with Townsville, Airlie Beach and
  Rockhampton within reach
- **Good access to industrial machining** from the surrounding industries

## The tide is the local factor

Mackay has a tidal range of several metres. That has practical consequences
that owners from other regions underestimate:

- **Strong tidal streams** mean boats work against real current, which is a
  load and a fuel consideration
- **Berthing and launching windows** are tide-dependent
- **Silt is mobilised** on every tide, so raw water intakes see more suspended
  material than in a low-range harbour
- **Hardstand and slipway operations** are scheduled around tides, which
  affects how a repower is programmed

## What local conditions do to the choice

**Turbid inshore water.** The combination of tidal range and river discharge
keeps the inshore water carrying sediment. Heat exchangers foul. Strainers need
clearing. On a workboat this is a serious argument for
[keel cooling](/guides/raw-water-vs-keel-cooling/).

**Tropical heat**, with the ventilation and corrosion consequences that come
with it.

**Cyclone season** from about November to April, shaping the yard calendar and
the operating pattern.

**Distance offshore to clean water.** Boats run out to the Whitsunday and
Cumberland groups, which is real distance for a trailer boat and a sensible
redundancy argument for anything larger.

## What to check before you commit

- Whether the yard's programme accounts for tidal access
- Strainer and heat exchanger access, given sediment-laden water
- Parts availability in the region for the engine family
- Timing against cyclone season
- Comparison quotes from Airlie Beach or Townsville on a large job

## Frequently asked questions

**Where do I buy a marine diesel engine in Mackay?**
The Mackay Marina and harbour precinct holds the local capability, with
Townsville, Airlie Beach and Rockhampton within a few hours for dealer breadth.

**Does the tidal range affect engine work?**
Yes. Slipway and hardstand access is tide-dependent, and the programme for a
repower has to account for it.

**Why do my strainers clog so often here?**
A large tidal range keeps sediment in suspension in the inshore water. It is a
local condition rather than a fault.

**Is keel cooling worth it for a Mackay workboat?**
For a vessel operating constantly in sediment-laden inshore water, it removes
the component that causes most of the trouble.

**When should I plan major engine work?**
Outside cyclone season, and with the yard's tidal access programme understood
from the start.
