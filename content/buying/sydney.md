# Buy a marine diesel engine in Sydney

Sydney has the largest recreational fleet in the country and, for a market that
size, a surprisingly awkward set of physical constraints on getting an engine
into a boat.

## What the local market looks like

Dealer coverage is good. Most major brands are represented across the basin,
and there is a deep independent marine engineering trade around Pittwater,
Middle Harbour, Rozelle Bay and Botany.

What Sydney does not have in abundance is space. Hardstand is expensive and
booked out, travel lift slots are contested, and the yards that can lift a
larger boat are concentrated in a few locations. On the Gold Coast the removal
facility is a given; in Sydney it is often the thing that determines when your
job happens and what it costs.

The market splits geographically in a way worth knowing:

- **Pittwater** — the largest cruising and sailing concentration, with a strong
  trade in sailboat auxiliaries and saildrive work
- **Middle Harbour and the lower Harbour** — mixed, with marina berths and
  swing moorings and limited on-site work
- **Rozelle Bay and Blackwattle** — commercial and workboat capability
- **Botany and the Georges River** — trailer boats, sportfishers and a
  different service trade again

## Moorings change the job

A large share of Sydney boats live on swing moorings rather than in marina
berths. That is fine for servicing, which a mobile mechanic does from a tender,
and it is a real complication for a repower: the boat has to be moved to a
facility, and that facility has to have a slot.

Factor the shift and the yard time into the quote from the beginning. It is a
line item that does not exist for an owner in a marina berth with a crane
alongside.

## What local conditions do to the choice

Sydney water is temperate and reasonably clean, so the cooling side is less
punishing than the tropics. The harder condition is exposure: boats here cross
the Heads into open ocean swell regularly, and an engine that is only ever
asked for cruise revs on flat water is a different proposition from one that
has to hold power in a seaway with the boat pitching.

Two consequences for buying:

- **Fuel pickup and filtration matter.** Motion stirs a tank, and a boat that
  runs fine on the harbour can find its filters in a southerly change. Specify
  a decent primary water-separating filter with a vacuum gauge.
- **Do not under-power.** An engine chosen at the bottom of the design range is
  the one that will be working hardest on the run home.

## Winter is your friend

Sydney's boating year has a real seasonal shape. Yard availability, workshop
capacity and pricing are all better in the cooler months, and a repower booked
for July is a different negotiation from one wanted in November.

## What to check before you commit

- Yard and travel lift availability, in writing, with dates
- Whether the mooring-to-yard delivery is included
- Access route: many Sydney boats need the engine out through a saloon
- Whether the exhaust and instruments are in the quote
- Council and marina restrictions on where work can be carried out

## Frequently asked questions

**Where do I buy a marine diesel engine in Sydney?**
All the major brands have dealer representation across the basin, with
independent marine engineering concentrated around Pittwater, Middle Harbour,
Rozelle Bay and Botany.

**Why is a Sydney repower more expensive than I expected?**
Hardstand and travel lift capacity is scarce and expensive here, and a boat on
a swing mooring has to be delivered to a facility. Both are real costs that do
not appear in the engine price.

**How far ahead should I book?**
Well ahead for anything requiring a lift, and aim for the cooler months when
yards have capacity.

**Does my boat need to come out of the water for a repower?**
Not always — it depends on the removal route and whether shaft work is
involved. Establish this early because it changes the cost substantially.

**What should I specify for offshore use out of the Heads?**
Good primary fuel filtration with a vacuum gauge, and an engine chosen with
margin rather than at the bottom of the design power range.
