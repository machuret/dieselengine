# Diesel Mechanics in Brisbane

Brisbane has one of the highest rates of 4WD and dual-cab ute ownership of any
Australian capital, and — more to the point — a very high proportion of those
vehicles actually get used for what they were built for. Towing caravans and
boats north, running to the Sunshine Coast and the Gold Coast, heading west for
work. That changes the fault profile substantially.

Towing is the defining Brisbane diesel variable. It raises exhaust gas
temperature, oil temperature and cylinder pressure well beyond what the standard
service schedule assumes. Turbo wear, head gasket failure and transmission-linked
overheating all arrive earlier on a vehicle that tows regularly, and Brisbane's
heat and humidity compound every one of them.

## Choosing a workshop in Brisbane

**Tell them you tow, before they quote.** It changes the diagnosis, the service
schedule and often the parts recommendation. A workshop that doesn't ask is a
workshop that will miss towing-related wear.
→ [Towing and diesel engine wear](/guides/towing-and-diesel-engine-wear/)

**Cooling system capability matters here.** Brisbane summers plus towing put
real load on cooling. A workshop that pressure-tests and combustion-gas-tests
properly will find a marginal head gasket before it strands you.
→ [Diesel engine overheating](/diesel-problems/diesel-overheating/)

**Check what they say about modifications.** Brisbane's 4WD scene means many
vehicles arrive with aftermarket intakes, exhausts, tunes and cooling. A good
workshop will tell you which modifications are causing your fault; a poor one
either blames all of them or ignores all of them.

## Queensland rules that affect you

**Safety certificates.** Queensland requires a safety certificate when a
registered vehicle is sold, not annually for light vehicles. As in Victoria,
that means deferred maintenance can accumulate — relevant if you are buying.

**Emissions.** DPF removal on a road-registered vehicle is not legal, and
Queensland enforces smoky vehicle provisions. Any workshop offering it is
offering to make your vehicle non-compliant, and that has insurance
implications as well as legal ones.
→ [Diesel emissions rules in Australia](/guides/diesel-emissions-rules-australia/)

**Modifications and ADR compliance.** Queensland has a defined framework for
vehicle modifications. Engine and driveline changes, including some performance
tuning, can affect compliance and insurance.
→ [ADR compliance for engine modifications](/guides/adr-compliance-engine-modifications/)

## Frequently asked questions

**How often should I service a diesel that tows regularly?**
More often than the book says. Towing is severe-duty operation, and most
manufacturers define a shortened interval for it that owners never see because
the standard schedule is what gets printed in the service book.
→ [Towing and diesel engine wear](/guides/towing-and-diesel-engine-wear/)

**Does Queensland require an annual diesel inspection?**
No — a safety certificate is required when a vehicle is sold, not annually for
light vehicles.

**Will a DPF delete pass a Queensland safety certificate?**
DPF removal on a road-registered vehicle is not legal in Australia, regardless
of what any individual inspection does or does not check. It also has insurance
consequences.

**My 4WD overheats when towing up the range — is that normal?**
No. Climbing under load is exactly when a marginal cooling system reveals
itself. Get it tested before it becomes a head gasket.
→ [Diesel engine overheating](/diesel-problems/diesel-overheating/)
