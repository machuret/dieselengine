# Marine diesel engines for Princess yachts

Princess is a British builder with a substantial Australian presence in the
larger flybridge and V-class ranges, and the boats are engineered to a high
standard with correspondingly high expectations of servicing.

## The usual arrangement

Princess yachts carry **twin shaft-driven diesels** across most of the range,
with **Volvo Penta, Caterpillar and MAN** all well represented depending on
model and year. Volvo Penta IPS appears on parts of the more recent range.

The plates are the answer — the same hull was offered with different packages.

## What matters on these boats

**They are complex.** A modern Princess carries integrated systems — electronic
engine management, proprietary display and control networks, hydraulics,
stabilisers on many boats — and the engine is one node in that. Diagnosing an
engine fault sometimes means diagnosing the network around it.

That has a practical consequence: **the dealer or a technician with the right
diagnostic tools is worth more on these boats than on a simpler one.** Ask about
diagnostic capability before booking work.

**MAN and Caterpillar support in Australia.** Both are well supported, but the
depth varies by region. If your boat is based outside a capital, confirm who can
service it locally before you need to know.

**Stabilisers, thrusters and hydraulics** are frequently driven off the main
engines and are part of the same service conversation.

**Sustained cruise load.** These boats deliver real performance and get used for
it. Cooling and fuel systems work.

## If it has IPS

Pod drives are proprietary and dealer-serviced, with their own scheduled
maintenance and haul-out requirements. This is a significant and separate line
in the ownership cost, and it constrains where in Australia you can have work
done. See [shaft vs sterndrive vs IPS](/guides/shaft-vs-sterndrive-vs-ips/).

## Repowering a Princess

A major project on any boat of this size, and one where the integration is the
hard part — panels, networks, controls and displays as much as the engines.

Get the quote itemised, and confirm who will handle the systems integration
rather than just the mechanical installation.

## Frequently asked questions

**What engines are in Princess yachts?**
Twin shaft diesels — Volvo Penta, Caterpillar or MAN depending on model and
year — with Volvo Penta IPS on parts of the recent range.

**Do I need a dealer to service a Princess?**
For electronic engine and network diagnostics, a technician with the right tools
matters more than on a simpler boat. Confirm capability before booking.

**Is MAN well supported in Australia?**
It is supported, with depth varying by region. If you are based outside a
capital, establish local capability before you need it.

**What does IPS add to running costs?**
Scheduled pod maintenance requiring haul-out and, in practice, a dealer or
trained technician. It is a separate and significant line.

**What makes a Princess repower complex?**
Systems integration — panels, networks, controls and displays — as much as the
mechanical installation.
