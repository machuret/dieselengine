# Marine diesel engines for Maritimo boats

Maritimo builds long-range motor yachts on the Gold Coast with a deliberate
engineering philosophy, and that philosophy is visible the moment you open the
engine room.

## The usual arrangement

Maritimo has stayed committed to **twin shaft drive** across its range while
much of the market moved to pods, and the boats are delivered with
**Volvo Penta, Cummins, Caterpillar or Scania** depending on model and year.

Confirm yours from the plates.

## The shaft drive decision matters to owners

Maritimo's use of shaft drive rather than pods is not incidental — it is central
to what the boats are for. The consequences for an owner:

**Serviceable anywhere.** Nothing below the waterline is proprietary. Any
competent marine mechanic in any Australian port can work on the drivetrain,
which is exactly what a boat intended for long coastal passages needs. Compare
that with a pod boat needing a dealer with the manufacturer's tool.

**Fewer scheduled below-waterline items.** No bellows, no leg service intervals.
The stern gland and cutless bearing are the wear items.

**Robust to grounding** in the sense that damage tends to be to shaft, prop or
strut rather than to a drive unit.

**Less low-speed manoeuvrability** without thrusters, which is why these boats
generally carry them.

## What comes up

**Engine room access.** Maritimo is unusual among production builders in
providing walk-in engine room access on much of the range, often with a
direct entry from the aft accommodation. That is a real saving on every job for
the life of the boat, and it is worth knowing when comparing service quotes
across brands.

**Long-range running.** These boats do genuine passages. Sustained cruise load
means the raw water side works and the fuel system matters. Filtration and tank
condition deserve the attention that a boat crossing to Tasmania or running up
the Queensland coast implies.

**Alignment.** Shaft-driven boats need periodic alignment checks, and after any
grounding or mount replacement.

**Twin everything.** Servicing is per engine.

## Repowering a Maritimo

Shaft drive keeps the options relatively open compared with a pod or saildrive
boat, and the engine room access reduces the removal cost that dominates many
repower quotes.

Establish scope on shafts, propellers, exhaust, panels and looms, and whether
the gearboxes are included.

## Frequently asked questions

**What engines are in Maritimo boats?**
Twin shaft diesels — Volvo Penta, Cummins, Caterpillar or Scania depending on
model and year. The plates are the authoritative answer.

**Why does Maritimo use shaft drive instead of pods?**
Simplicity, serviceability anywhere, and robustness for long-range coastal
work. It suits what the boats are built to do.

**Is engine access good on a Maritimo?**
Unusually good on much of the range, with walk-in engine rooms. That reduces
labour on essentially every job.

**Do Maritimos need alignment checks?**
Yes, as any shaft-driven boat does — periodically, and after a grounding or new
mounts.

**What should I watch on a long-range Maritimo?**
Fuel system condition and filtration, and the raw water side. Sustained cruise
passages are what these boats do, and both work hard.
