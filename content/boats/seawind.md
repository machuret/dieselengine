# Marine diesel engines for Seawind catamarans

Seawind is the Australian-designed cruising catamaran, and the boats were
conceived for the conditions they are sailed in here — which shows in the
engineering.

## The usual arrangement

Twin engines, one per hull, commonly **Yanmar or Volvo Penta**, on saildrives
across much of the range. Earlier and smaller models in the fleet have used
other arrangements including outboard auxiliaries on the smallest boats.

Confirm from the plates.

## Designed for these waters

The advantage of an Australian-conceived cat is that the engine installation was
specified with warm water, hot engine bays and long coastal passages in mind
rather than adapted to them. Ventilation, cooling margin and access were
considered against the conditions the boats actually meet.

That does not make servicing optional. It does mean the boats tend not to have
the marginal engine bay heat problems that some European imports show in a
Queensland summer.

## What comes up

**The usual twin-saildrive items** — two diaphragms with time-based intervals,
two sets of leg anodes in warm water, two lower seals.

**Coastal passage use.** Seawinds are sailed to the Whitsundays, up the
Queensland coast and offshore. That means sustained motoring in calms and real
load punching into a chop. Fuel filtration and cooling both matter.

**Charging hours.** As on any cruising cat, engines run for power as well as
propulsion. Low-load hours accumulate; give them real load regularly.

**Local support.** Being an Australian brand, boat-specific knowledge and parts
are considerably easier than for imported cats — a genuine practical advantage.

## Repowering a Seawind

Twin saildrive installation, so both engines and both legs are in scope, and the
drive family constrains the engine choice.

## Frequently asked questions

**What engines do Seawind catamarans have?**
Twin Yanmar or Volvo Penta saildrive auxiliaries across much of the range;
smaller and earlier models vary. Check both plates.

**Are Seawinds better suited to Australian conditions?**
They were designed here for these waters, so engine bay ventilation and cooling
margin were specified against local conditions rather than adapted.

**Is support easier than for an imported cat?**
Yes. Boat-specific knowledge and parts are more readily available for an
Australian brand.

**Do the saildrives need the same attention?**
Exactly the same — two diaphragms on time-based intervals, two sets of leg
anodes, two lower seals.

**What should I watch on a coastal-cruising Seawind?**
Fuel filtration and tank condition, and the raw water side. Long passages and
sustained motoring work both.
