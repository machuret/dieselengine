# Loses power under load

The engine runs fine at the dock and falls away when you ask it to work. That
pattern is diagnostic in itself: almost everything that behaves this way is a
restriction that only matters at high demand.

## Is it safe to keep going?

At reduced load, usually. But losing power offshore is how a manageable problem
becomes a rescue, so treat it as a reason to head in rather than to press on.

Shut down if it comes with overheating, smoke or unusual noise.

## Likely causes, ranked — cheapest first

| Cause | How common | Confirms it | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fouled bottom or damaged propeller** | **Very common** | Will not reach rated WOT RPM | Antifoul |
| Blocked fuel filters | Very common | Filter bowl; vacuum gauge rising | Filters |
| Blocked tank vent | Common | Opening the filler cap restores power | Free |
| Air leak on the fuel suction side | Common | Perished bowl seal, loose fitting | Cheap |
| Collapsing suction hose | Common | Fine at idle, fails at revs | Hose |
| Blocked exhaust elbow | Common | Back pressure, soot, heat | Elbow |
| Dirty air filter | Common | Inspection | Clean |
| Turbo or aftercooler fault | Occasional | Low boost | Repair |
| Slipping gearbox | Occasional | Revs rise, speed does not | Gearbox |
| Worn injectors or pump | Occasional | Bench test | Specialist |
| Low compression | Less common | Leak-down | Major |

**The tank vent one is worth knowing.** A blocked vent creates a vacuum in the
tank as fuel is drawn out, progressively starving the engine over an hour or
two. Opening the filler cap fixes it instantly — which is also how you diagnose
it.

## What you can check yourself

- Does the engine reach rated WOT RPM? On a clean bottom?
- Open the fuel filler cap while it is struggling — does it improve?
- Filter and separator condition; vacuum gauge if fitted
- Air filter and intake
- Does it smoke black at load? That is overload or air restriction.
- Do the revs rise without matching speed? That is the gearbox or the prop.

## When to call a mechanic

Once the bottom, prop, filters and vent are ruled out. Bring the actual WOT RPM
figure — it narrows the diagnosis substantially.

## Frequently asked questions

**Fine at idle, gutless at revs — what does that suggest?**
A restriction that only matters at high demand: filters, a collapsing hose, a
blocked vent, or air on the suction side.

**Power fades over an hour then recovers after stopping — why?**
Classic blocked tank vent. The vacuum builds while running and releases when you
open the filler.

**Could it be the propeller?**
Yes. A damaged or fouled prop, or one that was never right, is a very common
cause and shows as low WOT RPM.

**Should I replace the injectors?**
Not until fuel supply, air and overload are ruled out. Injectors are the
expensive guess on this symptom.
