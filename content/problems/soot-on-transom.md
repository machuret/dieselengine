# Black soot on the transom

A black stain spreading from the exhaust outlet is unburnt fuel being deposited
on your hull. It is a cosmetic nuisance and a diagnostic signal — the engine is
producing more soot than it should, continuously.

## Is it safe to keep going?

Yes, but it is telling you something is wrong. Persistent sooting means
incomplete combustion, which means wasted fuel and, usually, an engine working
harder than it should.

## Likely causes, ranked

| Cause | How common | Confirms it |
|---|---|---|
| **Overloaded engine — fouled bottom or wrong prop** | **Very common** | Will not reach rated WOT RPM |
| Prolonged idling and light-load running | Very common | Usage pattern |
| Blocked exhaust elbow | Common | Power loss, back pressure |
| Dirty air filter or restricted intake | Common | Inspection |
| Turbo or aftercooler fault | Common | Low boost |
| Worn or leaking injectors | Occasional | Bench test |
| Injection timing | Occasional | Specialist |

## The idling trap

A boat that spends a lot of time idling — waiting at a bridge, drifting while
fishing, running a genset-less engine for charging — sooties its transom without
anything being mechanically wrong. Diesels burn cleanly at load and poorly at
idle.

If the soot is your only symptom and the engine reaches rated revs, run it under
proper load periodically. That is maintenance, not a repair.

## What you can check yourself

- Does it reach rated WOT RPM on a clean bottom?
- When was it last antifouled?
- Air filter and intake
- Filter and separator condition
- Your usage pattern — how much idling?

## When to call a mechanic

If the engine will not reach rated revs, if power has dropped, or if the sooting
appeared suddenly rather than building up.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is transom soot always a fault?**
No. Heavy idling produces it on a healthy engine. Combined with low revs or lost
power, it is a fault.

**How do I clean it off?**
A marine hull cleaner will lift it, but it returns until the cause is addressed.

**Would a new prop reduce it?**
If the current one is over-pitched, yes, markedly — the engine stops being
overloaded.

**Does sooting mean my injectors are worn?**
Possibly, but check overload and air restriction first. Injectors are the
expensive guess.
