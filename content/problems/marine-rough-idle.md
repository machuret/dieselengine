# Rough idle or uneven running

An engine that shakes, hunts or runs unevenly at idle is not firing the same in
every cylinder, or is not getting a steady fuel supply.

## Is it safe to keep going?

Usually yes at cruise, if it smooths out under load. But rough running is often
the early stage of something that gets worse, and if it is fuel-related it can
become a stoppage at an inconvenient moment.

Shut down if it comes with smoke, overheating or unusual noise.

## Likely causes, ranked

| Cause | How common | Confirms it | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Air in the fuel system | Very common | Bleeding smooths it | Cheap |
| Air leak on the suction side | Very common | Perished bowl seal, loose fitting | Cheap |
| Partially blocked filters | Very common | Filter bowl, vacuum gauge | Filters |
| Water or contamination in the fuel | Common | Tank bottom sample | Polish |
| One injector faulty | Common | Cylinder cut-out test | Injector |
| Damaged or fouled propeller | Common | Vibration, not misfire — feels similar | Prop |
| Failed damper plate | Common | Rattle at idle, gone in gear | Damper |
| Loose or perished engine mounts | Common | Excess movement at idle | Mounts |
| Low compression on a cylinder | Occasional | Leak-down test | Major |
| Injection timing | Occasional | Specialist | Specialist |

**Distinguish vibration from misfire.** A damaged propeller, a worn cutless
bearing or sagging engine mounts produce shaking that feels like rough running
but is entirely mechanical. If it changes with gear engagement or shaft speed
rather than with engine speed, look at the driveline.

## What you can check yourself

- Drain the separator; look for water and sludge
- Change filters and bleed properly
- Check the bowl seal and every suction-side fitting for air
- Note whether it is worse cold, worse hot, or constant
- Note whether it changes in gear — that points at the driveline or damper plate
- Check engine mounts for sag and movement

## When to call a mechanic

If fresh filters and bleeding do not fix it. Ask for a cylinder cut-out test
before injectors are replaced — it identifies which cylinder rather than
replacing the set on suspicion.

## Frequently asked questions

**Rough cold, smooth warm — what is that?**
Often glow plugs on smaller engines, or an injector that seals poorly cold.
Worth diagnosing before it becomes constant.

**It shakes but the engine sounds even — misfire or vibration?**
Vibration. Look at the propeller, cutless bearing, alignment and engine mounts
rather than the fuel system.

**Rattle at idle that disappears in gear?**
Classic failed damper plate between engine and gearbox.

**Should I just replace the injectors?**
Not on suspicion. A cut-out test identifies the cylinder; bench testing confirms
the injector.
