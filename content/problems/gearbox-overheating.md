# Marine gearbox overheating

A gearbox running hot is destroying its own oil, and once the oil has gone the
clutch packs and bearings follow quickly.

## Is it safe to keep going?

Reduce load and get in. A gearbox that overheats under load will fail under
load, and it will do it at the worst moment.

## Likely causes, ranked

| Cause | How common | Confirms it | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|

| Low oil level | Very common | Dipstick | Trivial |
| Wrong oil type | Common | Plate on the box | Oil change |
| Slipping clutches generating heat | Common | Slips as well as overheats | Rebuild |

| Misalignment loading the output bearing | Occasional | Vibration, seal leaks | Alignment |

**The cooler is the first place to look**, and it is easy to forget it exists. It
is a small raw-water heat exchanger with exactly the same enemies as the engine's
— a blocked strainer, a tired impeller, or marine growth. If your engine cooling
is marginal, the gearbox cooler is marginal too.

## What you can check yourself

- Gearbox oil level and condition. Burnt smell means it has been hot; milky
  means water from a perforated cooler.
- Raw water flow generally — strainer, seacock, impeller
- Does the engine reach rated WOT RPM? An overloaded driveline heats the box.
- Does it slip as well as overheat? Slipping generates its own heat.

## When to call a mechanic

If the cooler is clear and the oil is right and it still runs hot. Milky gearbox
oil means the cooler has perforated and seawater is entering — deal with that
promptly, because it destroys bearings.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does my gearbox have its own cooler?**
Most inboard installations do — a small raw-water heat exchanger. It blocks like
any other.

**Milky gearbox oil — what does that mean?**
Water has entered, usually through a perforated cooler. Address it promptly;
seawater destroys gears and bearings quickly.

**Can a fouled bottom overheat the gearbox?**
Yes. Overload puts more torque through the box for the same speed, and heat
follows.

**How often should gearbox oil be changed?**
On the manufacturer's interval, alongside an engine service. It is the most
commonly forgotten item on the drivetrain.
