# Nanni Diesel distributors in Australia

Nanni is a French marinizer working predominantly from Kubota industrial blocks,
and it appears in the Australian fleet mainly in imported European yachts and in
repowers.

{{DEALERS:nanni}}

## The marinizer model, again

As with Beta and Solé, the ownership case turns on one distinction:

**Base engine parts** are Kubota, supported through an Australian industrial and
agricultural network of enormous depth.

**Marine parts** — heat exchanger, marine manifold, sea water pump, panel, loom,
gearbox interface — are Nanni's own and come through the marine channel.

The saving on the first is real. The lead time on the second is the thing to
establish.

## Where you find them here

**Imported European yachts**, particularly French-built boats where Nanni was a
factory option.

**Repowers** of older yachts, where the conventional installation and mechanical
simplicity of much of the range suit a boat designed around a long-gone
auxiliary.

**Saildrive and shaft** installations both, depending on model.

## The network reality

Nanni's Australian presence is smaller than the volume brands'. That is not a
problem for a coastal boat with a mechanical engine — the base engine is
supported everywhere and the marine parts are orderable — but it is worth
planning around:

- Confirm where marine-specific parts are held before you need one
- Carry the marine spares that matter: impeller, cover gasket, belts, filters
- Keep your own records, because the next mechanic may not have seen one

For an offshore cruising boat this is the standard argument for a spares
inventory rather than a reason to avoid the brand.

## What to ask before booking

1. Which parts on this job are Kubota base engine and which are Nanni marine?
2. Where are Nanni marine parts held in Australia, and what is the lead time?
3. Has this workshop worked on one before?
4. If it is a saildrive, do you hold leg parts?
5. What panel and loom is fitted, and are spares available?

## Frequently asked questions

**Who distributes Nanni Diesel in Australia?**
Nanni operates through appointed marine points. The manufacturer's locator is
the authoritative list.

**Are Nanni engines Kubota-based?**
Much of the range is marinised Kubota. Base engine parts have wide Australian
support; marine componentry is Nanni's own.

**Is the smaller network a problem?**
Not for a coastal boat with a mechanical engine. For offshore cruising, carry
the marine-specific spares rather than relying on availability.

**Why do I see them in French yachts?**
Nanni was a factory option on a number of European builders' boats.

**What spares should I carry?**
Impeller and cover gasket, belts, filters, and any marine-specific item your
model is known to need.
