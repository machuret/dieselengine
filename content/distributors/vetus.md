# Vetus marine distributors in Australia

Vetus is unusual on this list because most Australian boat owners encounter the
brand through its equipment rather than its engines — and both come through the
same network.

{{DEALERS:vetus}}

## Two businesses, one badge

**Vetus engines** are marinised industrial blocks, Kubota-based across much of
the range, fitted in yachts and small craft and used in repowers.

**Vetus equipment** is the far larger presence in Australian boating: bow
thrusters, exhaust system components, waterlocks, hoses, seacocks, fuel and
water system fittings, hatches and ventilation. A great many boats with no Vetus
engine at all have a hull full of Vetus parts.

For an owner that means the network you deal with is often an equipment
distributor and chandlery channel as much as an engine service network.

## Why the exhaust componentry matters

Vetus waterlocks, exhaust hose, gooseneck fittings and anti-siphon devices are
fitted across an enormous range of Australian boats regardless of engine brand.

These are not trivial components:

- **The waterlock** stops water running back to the engine when it stops
- **The anti-siphon or vented loop** prevents seawater siphoning into the engine
  through the exhaust
- **Exhaust hose** has a service life and perishes

A failure in any of them can put water where it must not be. If you have ever
found water in a cylinder, this part of the system is where the investigation
goes — and the parts are readily available here.

## The engines

Kubota-based across much of the range, which means the same split as Beta and
Nanni: base engine parts through a wide industrial network, marine componentry
through the marine channel.

Vetus engines are less common in Australia than Beta or Yanmar, so confirm
marine parts availability and lead time before relying on it.

## What to ask before booking

1. Is this an engine service point or an equipment distributor?
2. For engine work — which parts are base engine and which are Vetus marine?
3. Are exhaust system components in stock?
4. What is the age and condition of my exhaust hose and waterlock?
5. Is my anti-siphon arrangement correct for the installation?

## Frequently asked questions

**Who distributes Vetus in Australia?**
Vetus operates through appointed distribution and dealer channels covering both
engines and the much larger equipment range.

**Are Vetus engines Kubota-based?**
Much of the range is. Base engine parts have wide industrial support; marine
componentry comes through the marine channel.

**Why do I have Vetus parts if my engine is a Yanmar?**
Vetus exhaust components, waterlocks, thrusters and fittings are used across the
industry regardless of engine brand.

**How important is the waterlock and anti-siphon?**
They stop seawater reaching the engine through the exhaust. A failure can put
water in a cylinder, which is a serious and expensive event.

**Does exhaust hose need replacing?**
It has a service life and perishes. On an older boat it is worth inspecting
properly rather than assuming.
