# Iveco and FPT marine distributors in Australia

FPT Industrial, the engine business within the Iveco group, supplies marine
engines in Australia through an arrangement that — like Scania's — sits
alongside a commercial vehicle network.

{{DEALERS:iveco-fpt-marine}}

## Where they appear in the Australian fleet

**Commercial vessels** — charter, ferries, workboats — where the ratings and the
parts logistics suit an operator.

**Imported motor yachts**, particularly Italian-built boats where FPT was a
factory choice.

**Mercury Diesel packages**, which have used FPT-derived blocks. If you have a
Mercury Diesel sterndrive, the base engine question may lead here even though
you deal with the Mercury network.

That last point catches people out. Establish which network holds which parts
for your specific package.

## The commercial vehicle connection

Iveco's Australian presence supports trucks and buses, and the marine engines are
relatives of what that network handles.

**Advantage:** base engine parts and general diesel capability across a
footprint wider than a marine-only brand's.

**Limit:** marine-specific componentry — heat exchanger, marine aftercooler, sea
water pump, marine exhaust arrangement, gearbox interface — comes through the
marine channel, and the number of technicians who understand a wet exhaust and a
raw water circuit is far smaller than the number who understand the engine.

Ask for a marine-experienced technician rather than assuming.

## Duty ratings

FPT publishes ratings across recreational, light commercial and continuous duty.
For a charter or workboat this is not a detail — it affects warranty and, in
survey, the installation's suitability. See
[engine requirements for commercial vessel survey](/guides/commercial-vessel-survey-requirements/).

## What to ask before booking

1. Marine-appointed point, or a commercial vehicle branch?
2. Do you hold marine-specific parts, or only base engine?
3. If my engine came in a Mercury package, which network do I use?
4. Diagnostic access for my marine engine family?
5. What is the duty rating, and does it suit my operation?

## Frequently asked questions

**Who distributes FPT and Iveco marine engines in Australia?**
Through appointed marine points alongside the Iveco commercial vehicle network.
The manufacturer's locator is authoritative.

**My Mercury Diesel has an FPT block — who services it?**
Establish which network holds which parts for your package. Drive and package
support is Mercury's; some base engine items may lead to FPT.

**Can an Iveco truck branch service my boat engine?**
Base engine work often; wet exhaust, raw water and marine gearbox knowledge is
narrower. Ask for a marine-experienced technician.

**Do duty ratings matter?**
Substantially for commercial vessels — they affect warranty and survey.

**Are parts easy to get?**
Base engine items benefit from the commercial vehicle footprint. Marine
componentry is the part to confirm.
