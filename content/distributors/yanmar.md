# Yanmar marine engine distributors in Australia

Yanmar is the default sailboat auxiliary in Australia and a significant presence
in planing craft, which means the network is oriented around a very large
installed base of small and medium engines.

{{DEALERS:yanmar}}

## How the network is structured

Yanmar operates through an Australian marine organisation with an appointed
dealer and service network. Because the installed base of sailboat auxiliaries
is so large, coverage in cruising centres — Pittwater, the Whitsundays,
Mooloolaba, Fremantle, the Gippsland Lakes — is generally better than the
population of those places would suggest.

## Saildrives are the local specialty

A very large share of Australian Yanmar installations are saildrives in
production cruising yachts and catamarans. That shapes what the network does:

**Leg parts and diaphragms** are core stock rather than special order in most
sailing centres.

**Diaphragm replacement** is routine, scheduled, haul-out work that the trade
programmes around slipway availability.

**Leg anodes** in the alloys Yanmar specifies are a stocked item.

If you own a saildrive boat, this is the practical advantage of the brand here —
see [how a saildrive works](/guides/how-a-saildrive-works/).

## The industrial connection

Yanmar's marine engines share heritage with an industrial and agricultural
engine business that has a large Australian footprint. For some base-engine
items, that widens the parts picture beyond the marine chain.

It does not extend to marine-specific components — heat exchangers, exhaust
manifolds, raw water pumps, saildrive legs — which come through the marine
network.

## The older engines

The 1GM, 2GM and 3GM families are still working in large numbers in Australian
yachts decades on. Support for them remains real, and there is a deep body of
independent knowledge — many of these engines are entirely mechanical and a good
independent is often the better value on them.

## What to ask before booking

1. Authorised Yanmar dealer or independent?
2. Do you hold saildrive parts, or do they come from elsewhere?
3. Diagnostic capability for the electronic families?
4. Can you programme a haul-out for leg work?
5. Warranty authority on this engine?

## Frequently asked questions

**Who distributes Yanmar marine engines in Australia?**
Yanmar operates through an Australian marine organisation with an appointed
dealer network. The manufacturer's dealer locator is the authoritative list.

**Are Yanmar saildrive parts easy to get here?**
In sailing centres, generally yes — the installed base is large enough that leg
parts and diaphragms are routine stock.

**Can an independent service an older Yanmar?**
The GM-series and other mechanical engines are well within independent
capability, and there is deep local knowledge of them.

**Do Yanmar marine and industrial parts overlap?**
For some base-engine items. Marine-specific components come through the marine
network.

**How do I schedule saildrive diaphragm replacement?**
It is a haul-out job. Book it with the slipway and the workshop together — that
scheduling is normal practice in sailing centres here.
