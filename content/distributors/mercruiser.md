# Mercruiser and Mercury Diesel distributors in Australia

Mercury's Australian network is enormous, because it is built around outboards
and petrol sterndrives. The diesel side sits inside that network, and the
difference between the two matters when you are booking work.

{{DEALERS:mercruiser}}

## A large network, unevenly relevant

Mercury has one of the densest dealer footprints in Australian boating — almost
every trailer-boat town has one. But the majority of those dealers exist to
serve outboards and petrol sterndrives.

**Not every Mercury dealer is a diesel dealer.** Ask directly. A workshop that
sells and services outboards all day may have limited experience with a
common-rail diesel sterndrive package.

That is not a criticism of the network; it is a reason to be specific about what
you need.

## What Mercury Diesel actually is

Mercury's diesel packages have been built around blocks sourced from established
diesel manufacturers, marinised and packaged with Mercury drives and controls.
The engine and the drive come from different engineering traditions.

Practically, that means two support questions rather than one:

- **The engine side** — parts, diagnostics and knowledge for the base engine
- **The drive side** — sterndrive legs, bellows, gimbal bearings, transom
  assemblies, which is Mercury's own territory and where the dealer network is
  strongest

## The sterndrive reality

Most Mercury Diesel installations are sterndrives, which is the highest
maintenance drive arrangement in common use:

- **Bellows** are a time-based replacement item and failure floods the boat
- **Gimbal bearing and universal joints** are wear items in salt water
- **Anodes** go fast on a permanently moored boat
- **Leg service** is scheduled, not optional

The dealer network's strength on drives is genuinely useful here. See
[shaft vs sterndrive vs IPS](/guides/shaft-vs-sterndrive-vs-ips/) for the wider
ownership picture.

## What to ask before booking

1. Do you service Mercury **Diesel**, or only petrol and outboards?
2. Do you have diagnostic access for the diesel packages?
3. Do you hold drive parts — bellows, gimbal, anodes — in stock?
4. Warranty authority on the diesel range?
5. Can you do the leg service and the engine service together?

## Frequently asked questions

**Who distributes Mercruiser and Mercury Diesel in Australia?**
Mercury operates a large national dealer network. The diesel range sits within
it, but not every dealer services diesel — ask specifically.

**Is every Mercury dealer able to service my diesel sterndrive?**
No. Much of the network is oriented to outboards and petrol sterndrives. Confirm
diesel capability before booking.

**How often do bellows need replacing?**
At the manufacturer's time-based interval. A bellows failure sinks boats, so
unknown history should be treated as overdue.

**Are diesel sterndrives expensive to run?**
They carry the highest routine below-waterline maintenance of the common drive
arrangements, and the consequences of deferring it are severe.

**Where do the engines come from?**
Mercury's diesel packages are built around blocks from established diesel
manufacturers, marinised and packaged with Mercury drives and controls.
