# Marine Diesel Mechanics in Perth

Perth boats work in more exposed water than most of the Australian fleet. The
Indian Ocean swell, the consistent afternoon sea breeze, and the run to Rottnest
mean engines here are used under genuine load rather than pottering in sheltered
bays. That is good for the engines and hard on cooling systems.

The market splits three ways: the Swan River fleet, the ocean marinas at
Fremantle, Hillarys and Two Rocks, and the Mandurah canals and estuary system to
the south. They are separate service areas, and a Mandurah operator crossing to
Hillarys is charging you an hour each way.

Perth's other characteristic is isolation. Parts for less common engines come
from the eastern states, and lead time is a real factor in planning any major
job. Ask about parts availability before you authorise work, not after.

## Where the trade is

Perth's trade runs along the coast and up the river, and the ends of it do
different work.

**Fremantle** is the industrial and commercial end, with engineering capability
attached to the fishing and offshore industries. Heavier work, slipping and
machining.

**Hillarys** serves the large northern-suburbs recreational fleet from the boat
harbour.

**Mandurah** covers the Peel estuary and the southern fleet, with its own local
trade.

**Applecross** and the Swan River berths are served largely by mobile operators.

## The question to ask first

In most Australian cities the first question is availability. In Perth it is
**parts**.

Anything not held in Western Australia comes across the Nullarbor by road or by
air, and both cost days. A workshop that holds stock for your engine family is
worth more than one that quotes slightly cheaper and orders from the east coast.

Ask directly: do you hold parts for this engine, or do they come from
interstate, and what is the realistic turnaround?

That single question changes the outcome of more Perth jobs than any other.

## Mobile, workshop or slipway

Mobile attendance is straightforward across the marina-berthed fleet. Slipping
and hardstand cluster at Fremantle and Mandurah.

For anything requiring a part that is not local, establish the lead time before
the boat comes out of the water rather than after.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why do parts take so long in Perth?**
Most marine engine parts for less common brands are held in the eastern states.
Ask about lead time before authorising a job — it is often the longest part of
the timeline.

**Does running to Rottnest regularly affect servicing?**
It means the engine works at sustained load in open water, which is good for it
overall but harder on the raw water circuit. Keep impeller and strainer
intervals tight.

**Can sand damage my cooling system?**
Yes. Shallow-water and surf-zone operation draws sand through the intake, which
abrades impellers and settles in heat exchangers.

**Is Mandurah served by Perth operators?**
Some cover both, many don't. Mandurah has its own operators and it is usually
better to use them.
