# What Marine Engine Work Costs in Australia

Every price on this site is a range collected from Australian marine workshops,
dated, and split into parts and labour. We tell you what the number assumes,
because a marine quote without its assumptions is close to meaningless — the
same nominal job varies by a factor of several depending on access.

## What drives a marine engine bill

**Access, more than anything else.** The same impeller change is thirty minutes
on an open sterndrive installation and half a day on an engine boxed under a
saloon floor with a bulkhead in front of it. When you get a quote, the first
question a good mechanic asks is what the boat is — that is why.

**Where the work happens.** At the berth, on a mooring by tender, on the water
under way, or hauled out on the hard. Each step up adds cost, and haul-out is
charged separately from the engine work.

**Marine specification parts.** A marine alternator, starter or heat exchanger
is built for salt and vibration and priced accordingly. Fitting the automotive
equivalent is a false economy that usually fails within a season.

**What else gets found.** Marine faults cluster. An overheat that cooked the
exhaust hose means the hose too. A failed impeller means finding every fragment
downstream in the heat exchanger.

**Downtime, for commercial operators.** For a charter or survey vessel the
labour rate is rarely the dominant cost. Availability is.

## How to read a marine quote

A complete quote separates:

- **Parts**, itemised, with the brand or grade stated
- **Labour**, as hours at a stated rate — not a lump sum
- **Attendance**: travel, dock access, tender or on-water time
- **Haul-out**, if required, usually a separate line and often a separate
  supplier
- **Consumables** — oils, filters, impellers, gaskets, anodes
- **The authorisation threshold** above which they must call you

A single-number marine quote hides which of those is inflated, and access is
where the surprises live.

## When the cheap quote is the expensive one

The recurring pattern in marine work is a symptom treated instead of a cause. A
new impeller fitted without recovering the old one's fragments from the heat
exchanger. An overheat cured by replacing a thermostat when the exhaust elbow
was blocking. A gearbox topped up rather than its cooler inspected.

The diagnostic hour is the cheapest hour on a marine invoice.

## Is it worth fixing?

At some point a rebuild competes with a repower — and a repower is never just an
engine. It involves beds, mounts, controls, instruments, shaft alignment and
sometimes the exhaust system.

## Frequently asked questions

**What do marine mechanics charge per hour in Australia?**
Rates run above automotive, and vary by location and by whether attendance is at
a berth, on a mooring or on the water. Each location page carries the local band.

**Why is my quote so much higher than the range here?**
Usually access. A range assumes reasonable engine bay access; a boxed-in engine
under a saloon sole can multiply the labour. Ask for the hours to be itemised.

**Is haul-out included in these prices?**
No. Slipway and haul-out are quoted separately, often by a different supplier.

**Do I pay for diagnosis if I don't proceed?**
Yes — diagnostic time is work performed. Many operators credit it against the
repair if you go ahead. Confirm at booking.

**Are these prices GST inclusive?**
Yes, unless a page says otherwise.
