# What Diesel Repairs Cost in Australia

Every price on this site is a range collected from Australian workshops, dated,
and split into parts and labour. We tell you what the number assumes, because a
repair cost without its assumptions is just a number.

## What drives a diesel repair bill

Four factors explain most of the variation between quotes for the same job:

**Labour rate.** Independent workshops and dealers differ substantially, and
rates vary by city — Sydney and Perth sit above the national average, and
mining-adjacent regions higher again because workshops compete with mine sites
for technicians.

**Vehicle class.** The same nominal job on a 4WD, a light commercial and a truck
are different jobs. Access, component size, fluid volumes and lifting equipment
all change. Our tables split by class rather than quoting one figure.

**Parts source.** Genuine, OEM-equivalent and aftermarket can differ by a
multiple on the same component. For precision fuel-system parts this is not a
place to economise; for ancillaries it often is.

**What else gets found.** Diesel repairs cluster. A turbo failure that put oil
through the intercooler means cleaning the intercooler too. Budget for the
related work, not just the headline part.

## How to read a quote

A complete quote separates:

- **Parts**, itemised, with the brand or grade stated
- **Labour**, as hours at a stated rate — not a lump sum
- **Consumables** — fluids, gaskets, filters
- **Diagnostic time**, and whether it is credited if you proceed
- **The authorisation threshold** — the figure above which they must call you
  before continuing

A single-number quote hides which of those is inflated. Ask for the breakdown;
a workshop that won't provide one is telling you something.

→ [How to compare diesel repair quotes](/guides/questions-to-ask-a-diesel-workshop/)

## When the cheap quote is the expensive one

The recurring pattern in diesel repair is a part replaced without the fault
being found. Injectors fitted to cure smoke that was actually a boost leak. A
turbo replaced because a code pointed at it, when a blocked DPF caused the code.
The second workshop then charges you for the correct repair, and you have paid
twice.

Diagnostic time on a modern diesel is not padding. It is the part of the bill
that determines whether the rest of it was necessary.

## Is it worth fixing?

At some point the repair exceeds what the vehicle is worth, or a rebuild becomes

## Frequently asked questions

**How much do diesel mechanics charge per hour in Australia?**
Rates vary by city and by whether the workshop is a dealer or independent. Each
city page carries the local band, dated.

**Why is my quote so much higher than the range on this page?**
Usually one of: your engine is an outlier for parts cost, the job includes
related work the range excludes, or the quote is from a dealer where our range
assumes an independent. Ask for the itemised breakdown and compare line by line.

**Do I have to pay for a diagnosis if I don't go ahead with the repair?**
Yes — diagnostic time is work performed. Many workshops credit it against the
repair if you proceed. Confirm the policy when you book.

**Can a workshop charge more than the quote?**
Not for the quoted work without your authorisation. A quote and an estimate are
treated differently, and you should know which you have been given.
→ [Your repair warranty rights](/guides/diesel-repair-warranty-rights-australia/)

**Are the prices on this site GST inclusive?**
Yes, all figures include GST unless a page says otherwise.
