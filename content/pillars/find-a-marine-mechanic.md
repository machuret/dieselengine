## Marine work is several different trades

Getting this wrong is the most common way boat owners waste money.

**Marine diesel mechanic / marine engineer.** Servicing, diagnosis, cooling
system work, fuel systems, most repairs. Your first stop for anything you can't
identify.
→ [Marine diesel mechanic](/services/marine-diesel-mechanic/)

**Marine electrician.** Charging systems, alarms, instrumentation, and the
corroded connections behind a surprising share of "engine" faults. Overlaps with
mechanics but is its own trade.
→ [Marine engine electrical](/services/marine-engine-electrical/)

**Diesel fuel injection specialist.** Tests and calibrates injectors and pumps
on a bench. Usually the same shops that serve trucks — the injection hardware is
often shared.
→ [Marine injector testing and replacement](/services/marine-injector-service/)

**Transmission specialist.** ZF, Twin Disc and Hurth gearboxes are rebuilt by
specialists, not by general workshops.
→ [Marine gearbox service](/services/marine-gearbox-service/)

**Shipwright / slipway.** Anything below the waterline: shafts, cutless
bearings, propellers, skin fittings, and haul-out.
→ [Slipway and haul-out engine work](/services/slipway-haul-out/) ·
[Propeller shaft service](/services/propeller-shaft-service/)

**Engine reconditioner.** Machining, rebuilds, exchange engines.
→ [Marine engine rebuild](/services/marine-engine-rebuild/)

**Marine surveyor.** Independent condition assessment, usually pre-purchase.
A surveyor is not a mechanic and does not do repairs — which is exactly why
their assessment is worth having.
→ [Marine engine survey](/services/marine-engine-survey/)

## Dock visit, workshop, or haul-out?

**Mobile / dock visit** covers servicing, diagnosis, impellers, filters, anodes
above the waterline, electrical, alarms and most routine work. Most marine
mechanics work this way by default — the boat rarely comes to them.
→ [Mobile marine mechanic](/services/mobile-marine-mechanic/)

**Workshop** is needed for bench work: injectors, pumps, gearbox rebuilds,
turbochargers, cylinder heads, engine reconditioning.

**Haul-out / slipway** is needed for anything below the waterline — shaft,
cutless bearing, propeller, skin fittings, saildrive seals, and hull anodes.

**On-water emergency** is its own service, priced accordingly.
→ [Emergency and on-water breakdown repair](/services/emergency-marine-breakdown/)

## How to choose

1. **Are they experienced on your engine?** Ask directly: "how many Volvo Penta
   D4s do you see a month?" Marine brands differ enormously and dealer-level
   diagnostic software is brand-specific.
2. **Can they get to your boat?** A mechanic without access to your marina, or
   without a tender for a mooring, is not an option regardless of how good they
   are.
3. **Do they have the diagnostic tools for your engine?** Modern electronic
   marine diesels — Volvo Penta EVC, Cummins, Caterpillar, Yanmar — need
   manufacturer-level software. A generic reader will not do it.
4. **Will they quote with parts and labour separated?**
5. **What is the authorisation threshold** above which they must call you?
6. **For a commercial vessel:** do they understand your survey obligations and
   will their paperwork satisfy them?

## Your rights

Repairs in Australia carry consumer guarantees that cannot be contracted away:
work must be carried out with due care and skill, and parts must be fit for
purpose. A workshop cannot charge you for unauthorised additional work.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do marine mechanics come to the boat?**
Usually, yes — most marine diesel work happens at the berth. Bench work and
haul-out jobs are the exceptions.

**Do I need a dealer, or will an independent do?**
Independents are frequently better value and often more experienced on older
engines. Dealers hold the advantage on new engines under warranty and on faults
needing factory software or a software update.

**How do I find someone for an engine nobody has heard of?**
Start from the base engine. Many yacht auxiliaries are Kubota industrial blocks
under a marine cover, which widens your options considerably.
→ Marine diesel engine brands

**Can a truck diesel mechanic work on my boat?**
For the fuel injection side, sometimes — the hardware overlaps. For cooling,
exhaust, corrosion, gearboxes and drives, no.

**What does a call-out cost?**
Marine call-out is normally charged as travel plus labour, higher for on-water
attendance. Each location page carries the local band.
