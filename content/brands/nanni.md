# Nanni Diesel marine engines in Australia

Nanni is a French marinizer working predominantly from Kubota industrial blocks,
appearing in the Australian fleet mainly in imported European yachts and in
repowers.

## Where you find them

**N2.10, N3.21 and N4.38** — cruising yacht auxiliaries, on saildrive and shaft.

**T4.155** and the larger range — higher-output installations.

Common as a factory option in French-built yachts, and chosen for repowers where
a conventional, mechanically simple engine suits a boat designed around a
long-gone auxiliary.

## The marinizer split

**Base engine parts** are Kubota, supported through an Australian industrial and
agricultural network of enormous depth — see
[Kubota marine base engines](/engine-brands/kubota-marine/).

**Marine parts** — heat exchanger, marine manifold, sea water pump, panel, loom,
gearbox interface, saildrive leg where fitted — are Nanni's own and come through
the marine channel.

The saving on the first is real. The lead time on the second is the thing to
establish before you need one. See [Nanni distributors](/distributors/nanni/).

## What owners should know

**The Australian network is smaller than the volume brands'.** Not a problem for
a coastal boat with a mechanical engine; worth planning around for offshore
cruising.

**Carry the marine spares that matter** — impeller and cover gasket, belts,
filters — rather than relying on availability.

**Keep your own records.** On a less common engine, your documentation is what
the next mechanic works from.

## Frequently asked questions

**Are Nanni engines Kubota-based?**
Much of the range is marinised Kubota. Base engine parts have wide Australian
support; marine componentry is Nanni's own.

**Is the smaller network a problem?**
Not for a coastal boat with a mechanical engine. For offshore cruising, carry the
marine-specific spares.

**Why do I see them in French yachts?**
Nanni was a factory option on a number of European builders' boats.

**What spares should I carry?**
Impeller and cover gasket, belts, filters, and anything your specific model is
known to need.

**Which Kubota is underneath?**
Nanni's documentation, or the block casting, identifies the base engine family.
