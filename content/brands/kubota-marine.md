# Kubota marine base engines in Australia

Kubota does not sell marine engines. It makes the industrial blocks underneath a
large share of the small marine diesels in Australian yachts — Beta, Nanni,
Solé, Vetus and others — and understanding that is worth real money.

## The engines underneath

**V1305, V2203, D1105 and V3300** are among the families that appear, marinised
by different companies under different badges.

If your yacht has a Beta, a Nanni, a Solé or a Vetus auxiliary, roughly speaking
you own a Kubota with marine parts bolted to it.

## What is Kubota and what is not

**Kubota base engine** — block, crankshaft, pistons, valves, oil pump, injection
pump, injectors, timing components, oil filters and many gaskets. Supported
through an Australian network built for agriculture, construction and
groundcare, which is vastly larger than any marine one and reaches into country
towns with no marine trade at all.

**The marinizer's own parts** — heat exchanger, marine exhaust manifold, sea
water pump, wiring loom, instrument panel, gearbox adaptor, saildrive leg where
fitted. These come through the marine channel and are the slower, more expensive
half.

## What this means practically

**Ask which half a part belongs to.** A workshop quoting a full marine price for
an item available as a Kubota industrial part is either unaware or not saying.
You are entitled to ask.

**Identify the base engine.** Your marinizer's documentation, or the block
casting and plate, identifies the Kubota family — see
[how to identify your marine engine](/how/how-to-identify-your-marine-engine/).

**For remote cruising this is a genuine advantage.** Base engine servicing items
can be sourced in places a marine part cannot.

**Use the marinizer's service schedule.** Where it differs from Kubota's
industrial intervals, the marine schedule wins — it accounts for the raw water
circuit, the anodes and the salt that the industrial manual never considered.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does Kubota make marine engines?**
Not as such. It makes industrial blocks that marinizers such as Beta, Nanni,
Solé and Vetus convert for marine use.

**Can I buy Kubota parts for my Beta or Nanni?**
For base engine items frequently yes, through the industrial network. Marine
componentry comes through the marine channel.

**How do I find which Kubota is under my engine?**
The marinizer's documentation, or the block casting and plate.

**Which service schedule should I follow?**
The marinizer's. It accounts for the marine environment the industrial manual
does not.

**Is this an advantage for remote cruising?**
A real one. Base engine servicing items are available far more widely than
marine parts.
