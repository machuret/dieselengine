# Lombardini / Kohler Marine in Australia

Lombardini, now under Kohler, makes small industrial diesels that appear in
Australian yachts as compact auxiliaries and in generator sets.

## Where you find them

**LDW 502M, 1003M and 1404M** — small cruising yacht auxiliaries and generator
sets where space is tight.

## What owners should know

**Compact installations trade access for space.** These engines go into boats
that could not accept anything larger, which frequently means the back of the
engine, the exhaust elbow and the raw water pump are difficult to reach. Budget
more labour than the job sounds like.

**The marinizer split applies**, as with any marinised industrial engine: base
engine parts through the industrial network, marine componentry through the
marine channel.

**Generator sets are separate machines** with their own raw water circuit,
impeller, anodes and schedule — and the most neglected diesel on most boats. See
[marine generator sets](/engine-brands/marine-gensets/).

**Confirm parts lead time** before committing to a job. The Australian marine
network for this brand is modest.

## Plan around access

Before accepting a quote, ask which panels, joinery or generator enclosure must
be removed to reach the service point. A low parts price can still become an
expensive job when the raw-water pump or exhaust elbow faces a bulkhead. Take
wide photographs before anything is moved and record hose routing and belt
direction. For a generator, label it clearly as a genset rather than propulsion
engine when requesting parts; similar base engines can use different marine
cooling and control components.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where are Lombardini engines used?** Small cruising yacht auxiliaries and generator sets where space is tight.

**Is access an issue?** Frequently. These engines go where nothing larger fits, which often makes the back of the engine and the exhaust elbow hard to reach.

**Are parts easy to get?** The marine network is modest. Confirm lead time before booking work.

**Does my generator need separate servicing?** Yes. Its own raw water circuit, impeller, anodes and schedule.

**Can an independent service one?** The mechanical families, generally yes.
