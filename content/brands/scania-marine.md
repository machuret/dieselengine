# Scania Marine marine engines in Australia

Scania's Australian marine business sits inside the organisation that
supports its road transport customers, and for a boat owner that arrangement is
unusual and mostly favourable.

## Where you find them

**DI13 and DI16** — commercial vessels, ferries, charter and patrol craft, plus
larger Australian-built motor yachts including parts of the Maritimo range.

## What owners should know

**Parts logistics are excellent**, because road transport cannot wait for parts
and the inventory reflects that. Service response in the main centres is
oriented to minimising downtime.

**Engine knowledge is deep**, because the base engines are close relatives of
what the branch works on daily.

**Marine specialisation is narrower.** The number of people in the organisation
who understand a wet exhaust, a raw water circuit and a marine gearbox is much
smaller than the number who understand the engine. Ask for a marine-experienced
technician rather than assuming — see
[Scania distributors](/distributors/scania-marine/).

**Marine componentry comes through the marine channel** — heat exchanger,
marine-configuration aftercooler, sea water pump, exhaust arrangement, gearbox
interface.

## Give the branch the marine context

When booking, provide the engine serial number, duty rating, vessel use and
whether the fault involves the base engine or its marine systems. A road-engine
technician may understand the fuel and control platform perfectly while still
needing a marine colleague for the raw-water, wet-exhaust or gearbox side. For
a twin installation, record comparable rpm, temperature and load data from
both engines before the visit.

## Frequently asked questions

**Are parts easy to get?** Parts logistics are strong because the network exists to keep commercial transport running. Marine-specific componentry is the part to confirm.

**Can any Scania branch service a marine engine?** Base engine work generally. Wet exhaust, raw water and marine gearbox knowledge is narrower — ask for a marine-experienced technician.

**Where are they used in Australia?** Commercial vessels and larger Australian-built recreational craft.

**Does the road-transport connection help?** On parts availability and response time, yes. On marine installation knowledge, it is the thing to check.

**What is the duty rating on my engine?** The plate carries it, and it matters for commercial operation and warranty.
