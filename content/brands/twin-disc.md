# Twin Disc marine gearboxes in Australia

Twin Disc sits at the commercial and performance end of the Australian gearbox
market, behind larger engines in workboats, charter vessels and serious motor
yachts.

## Where you find them

**MG-5050, MG-5075 and MG-5091** — larger recreational and commercial
installations.

**QuickShift** electronic control systems, which change how the boat handles at
low speed and add a diagnostic dimension to servicing.

## What owners should know

**Commercial orientation.** These boxes are specified where downtime costs money,
and the servicing culture around them reflects that — documented maintenance,
scheduled oil analysis, planned replacement rather than run-to-failure.

**Electronic control needs the tool.** QuickShift and similar systems are not
purely mechanical, and a workshop without diagnostic access is limited to the
hydraulics. Ask before booking.

**The thrust bearing takes the propeller's push.** Which is why alignment
matters, and why a box that has run with a misaligned shaft often fails at the
bearing rather than the clutches.

**Separate schedule, separate oil.** As with any marine gearbox, it is not part
of the engine service unless the quote says so.

## What comes up

Oil cooler corrosion letting seawater in; clutch wear on vessels doing heavy
low-speed manoeuvring; control system faults on the electronic boxes; and
alignment-driven bearing failures.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where are Twin Disc gearboxes used?**
Larger recreational, charter and commercial installations where the engine
output exceeds what smaller boxes handle.

**Does electronic control complicate servicing?**
It adds a diagnostic dimension. A workshop without access to the control system
is limited to the hydraulics.

**Is the gearbox included in an engine service?**
Usually not. Separate schedule, separate oil, often a separate specialist.

**Why do these boxes fail?**
Oil cooler corrosion, clutch wear from heavy manoeuvring, and alignment-driven
thrust bearing damage.

**Should I rebuild or exchange?**
Ask about exchange availability for your model — on common units it is often
faster and comes with warranty.
