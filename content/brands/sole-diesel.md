# Solé Diesel marine engines in Australia

Solé is a Spanish marinizer working from Mini and industrial blocks, appearing
in the Australian fleet in imported yachts and in repowers of small auxiliaries.

## Where you find them

**Mini 17, 29 and 44** — small cruising yacht auxiliaries.

**SDZ** series and the larger range.

Most often encountered as a factory fit in European-built yachts, or as a chosen
repower where a compact, mechanically simple auxiliary suits the installation.

## What owners should know

**The marinizer pattern applies.** Base engine parts follow the underlying
industrial block and its network; marine componentry — heat exchanger, manifold,
sea water pump, panel, loom — is Solé's own and comes through the marine
channel.

**The Australian network is thin.** This is the main practical consideration.
Establish where marine-specific parts are held and what the lead time is before
committing to a job, and carry spares for offshore work.

**Mechanical simplicity is the compensation.** Much of the range has no engine
management to integrate, which means a competent independent can work on it and
you are not dependent on software access.

**Keep records.** On an uncommon engine your own documentation is what the next
mechanic works from, and it is worth more than usual at resale.

## Build a useful parts record

Keep photographs of both identification plates, receipts with part numbers and
a copy of the correct operator manual aboard. Record which filters and impeller
were actually fitted rather than relying on a model-only catalogue search.
Repowers often retain parts of the previous installation, so the control panel,
fuel plumbing or exhaust may not match a standard package. That record makes
routine service easier for any competent independent workshop.

## Frequently asked questions

**Are Solé engines well supported in Australia?**
The network is thinner than for the volume brands. Confirm marine parts
availability and lead time before booking work.

**Can an independent work on one?**
Much of the range is mechanical with no software to license, so yes.

**What spares should I carry?**
Impeller and cover gasket, belts, filters, and anything specific to your model.

**Why do I see them in European yachts?**
Solé was a factory option on a number of European builders' boats.

**What is the base engine?**
Solé's documentation identifies it. Knowing it widens where base engine parts
can be sourced.
