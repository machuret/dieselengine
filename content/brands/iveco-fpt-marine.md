# Iveco / FPT Marine marine engines in Australia

FPT Industrial, the engine business within the Iveco group, supplies marine
engines in Australia alongside a commercial vehicle network.

## Where you find them

**NEF N40 and N60**, and the **Cursor** range — commercial vessels, ferries,
workboats, and imported motor yachts, particularly Italian-built.

FPT-derived blocks have also appeared in **Mercury Diesel** packages, which
catches people out: if you have a Mercury Diesel sterndrive, the base engine
question may lead here even though you deal with the Mercury network.

## What owners should know

**Two networks, one engine.** Base engine parts and general diesel capability
benefit from the commercial vehicle footprint. Marine-specific componentry —
heat exchanger, marine aftercooler, sea water pump, exhaust arrangement, gearbox
interface — comes through the marine channel.

**Ask for a marine-experienced technician.** The number of people who understand
a wet exhaust and a raw water circuit is far smaller than the number who
understand the engine. See
[Iveco and FPT distributors](/distributors/iveco-fpt-marine/).

**Duty ratings matter** for charter and survey vessels — see
[engine requirements for survey](/guides/commercial-vessel-survey-requirements/).

## Frequently asked questions

**My Mercury Diesel has an FPT block — who services it?** Establish which network holds which parts. Drive and package support is Mercury\u2019s; some base engine items may lead to FPT.

**Can an Iveco truck branch service my boat engine?** Base engine work often. Wet exhaust, raw water and marine gearbox knowledge is narrower.

**Do duty ratings matter?** Substantially for commercial vessels — they affect warranty and survey.

**Are parts easy to get?** Base engine items benefit from the commercial vehicle footprint. Marine componentry is the part to confirm.

**Where are they common here?** Commercial vessels and imported Italian-built motor yachts.
