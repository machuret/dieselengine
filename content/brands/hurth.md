# Hurth marine gearboxes in Australia

Hurth gearboxes are behind a great many older Australian cruising yachts and
small inboards, and the first thing owners want to know is whether they are
supported. They are.

## Where you find them

**HBW 50, 100 and 150** — the mechanical cone-clutch transmissions fitted behind
small and medium auxiliaries across decades of production cruisers.

## Still supported

Hurth was absorbed into the ZF Marine range, and the designs are generally still
supported through those channels — see [ZF marine gearboxes](/engine-brands/zf-marine/)
and [ZF distributors](/distributors/zf-marine/).

For an owner that means a Hurth box is a normal service item rather than an
orphan, which is not true of every component of that age.

## The cone clutch, and how to treat it

The HBW family uses a mechanical cone clutch engaged directly by the control
lever, rather than the hydraulic clutch packs of a larger box. That has
consequences:

**It is simple**, with fewer things to go wrong and no oil pump to fail.

**It is unforgiving of being slammed between gears at speed.** Engagement should
be made at idle, deliberately. Repeated harsh shifting is the commonest cause of
avoidable wear.

**Cable adjustment matters more than on a hydraulic box.** A control cable short
of full travel means the cone never fully engages, which slips and wears. It is
also the cheapest thing to check when engagement feels wrong.

## What to check

- **Oil type and level** — the plate on the box is the authority, and it is not
  always engine oil
- **Oil condition** — milky means water, usually a cooler; dark and burnt means
  slip
- **Control cable travel**, at the lever and at the box
- **Alignment**, which is the most common non-wear cause of failure on any
  marine gearbox

See [how to check and change marine gearbox oil](/how/how-to-check-and-change-marine-gearbox-oil/).

## Frequently asked questions

**Are Hurth gearboxes still supported?**
Yes. The designs were absorbed into the ZF Marine range and are generally still
supported through those channels.

**Why does my Hurth slip?**
Often a control cable short of full travel, low oil, or the wrong oil, before
clutch wear. Check the cheap causes first.

**Can I shift at speed?**
No. A cone clutch should be engaged at idle, deliberately. Harsh shifting is the
main avoidable cause of wear.

**What oil does it take?**
The plate on the box says. It is not always engine oil, and the wrong fluid
causes clutch problems.

**Is it worth rebuilding?**
Often, on a sound boat. Establish parts availability for your specific model
first.
