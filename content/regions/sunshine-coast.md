# Marine diesel on the Sunshine Coast

Mooloolaba is a game fishing port, and the local marine diesel trade is
organised around boats that work much harder than the average pleasure cruiser.

## A commercial duty cycle on recreational boats

A day's game fishing is a long run offshore at cruise, hours of low-speed
trolling, and a long run home. Repeat through a season. That is far closer to
light commercial operation than to a Sunday on the harbour.

Three consequences the local workshops see constantly:

**Hours accumulate fast.** An engine here reaches in three years what a harbour
cruiser takes fifteen to reach. The hour meter means something different on this
coast, and generally something better — hours under real load are kinder than
long idle periods.

**Duty rating mismatch.** A recreational-rated engine being used this way is
outside its specification, with warranty consequences that surface at exactly
the wrong moment.

**The trolling problem.** Extended hours at very low load, in gear, is the
pattern that glazes bores and builds carbon. It is inherent to the fishery, which
is why running the engines up properly on the way home matters more here than
anywhere else.

## The bars

The Mooloolah River bar and Noosa's are why engine reliability on this coast is a
safety question rather than an inconvenience. An engine that falters crossing a
bar is a different event from one that falters in a marina.

What follows: adequate power margin, serious primary fuel filtration with a
vacuum gauge, and cooling that copes with sustained load at low boat speed. Read
[marine diesel fuel quality and storage](/guides/marine-fuel-quality-australia/)
— a bar crossing stirs a tank comprehensively.

## The trade

Capability is concentrated at **Mooloolaba**, with the marina and hardstand, plus
a smaller service trade around **Noosa** and the Mooloolah River. It is a
specialised market rather than a broad one: sportfisher repowers, charter
operator servicing under survey, and bar-crossing boats.

Both Brisbane and the Gold Coast are within a couple of hours by road, so
sourcing is rarely the constraint. **Yard capacity often is**, and it tightens
ahead of the fishing season.

## Charter operators

A meaningful part of this fleet carries paying passengers, which makes it a
domestic commercial vessel with survey obligations covering the installation as
well as the engine. Speak to your surveyor before a repower, not after — see
[engine requirements for survey](/guides/commercial-vessel-survey-requirements/).

## Frequently asked questions

**Do high hours matter on a Sunshine Coast boat?**
Less than they would on a cruiser. Hours accumulated under real load are kinder
to an engine than long idle periods. Records matter more than the meter.

**What engine rating does a game boat need?**
Light commercial duty at minimum for serious use. Recreational ratings do not
match this operating pattern.

**Why is trolling hard on an engine?**
Extended low-load running glazes bores and builds carbon. Run the engines up
properly on the way home.

**What should I fit for bar crossings?**
Power margin, and a primary water-separating filter with a vacuum gauge so you
can see restriction building before it becomes a stall.

**When should I book work?**
Outside the peak fishing season, when yard and workshop capacity is easier to
get.
