# Marine diesel on the NSW South Coast

A working fishing coast where the recreational fleet shares the water, the
weather and the entrances with boats that go out in conditions it would not.

## What breaks here

**Cold-start problems.** This coast is markedly cooler than anywhere north of
Sydney. Cold water is easy on cooling systems and hard on starting — battery
capacity, glow arrangements and oil grade all matter more, and the first start
of the season is where the winter shows.

**Layup deterioration.** Much of the recreational fleet sits through the cooler
months. Oil degrading by acid and moisture, water condensing in fuel tanks,
impellers taking a set — none of it cares about the hour meter. This is the
dominant risk for a lightly used boat here, not wear.

**Bar-entrance fuel failures.** The Clyde and several of the region's river
entrances need respect, and a bar crossing stirs a tank. The same pattern as the
coast further north: fine alongside, filters blocked in the entrance.

**Real weather.** An exposed coast with a genuine southerly season. Boats work
in conditions that make power margin a safety item rather than a preference.

## Buying into a working fleet's standards

Ulladulla has a genuine commercial fishing harbour, and that is useful to a
recreational owner in ways that are easy to miss:

- The workshops are used to engines that have to **work**, not just start
- Duty ratings and hard-use maintenance are familiar territory
- Slipway capability exists because the fishing fleet needs it
- The advice tends to be practical rather than aspirational

The corresponding constraint: **capacity follows the fishing seasons.** Ask
about the yard diary before committing to a date, because the commercial fleet
has standing claims on it.

## The trade

**Ulladulla** carries the commercial harbour and the engineering that supports
it. **Batemans Bay** sits on the Clyde with a recreational and charter fleet and
a bar entrance.

Nowra and the Shoalhaven add capacity to the north. Canberra's engineering
trade, a couple of hours inland, is used more than outsiders expect. Sydney is
around four hours away.

## Booking

Before the season rather than during it, and around the fishing calendar.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the main risk for a lightly used boat here?**
Layup deterioration over a long cool season — oil, fuel, impellers and anodes
all degrade on the calendar regardless of hours.

**Why won't my engine start after winter?**
Cold water and months of sitting. Battery capacity, glow system condition and
oil grade are the usual causes.

**Is a commercial fishing town a good place to get work done?**
Generally yes. The workshops are used to engines that have to work, and the
advice is practical.

**What limits booking?**
The fishing seasons. The commercial fleet has standing claims on yard capacity.

**What about the bar at Batemans Bay?**
Treat it as any bar entrance: power margin and serious fuel filtration are not
optional.
