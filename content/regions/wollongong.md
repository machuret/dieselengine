# Marine diesel in Wollongong and Shellharbour

Small harbours on an exposed coast, an hour from the largest boating market in
the country, with an industrial engineering base most coastal towns would envy.

## What breaks here

**Fuel problems that only show offshore.** The harbours are compact and the
coast outside them is exposed. Boats go from sheltered to genuinely open water
within minutes, and a tank that behaves in the harbour finds its sludge in a
swell. Filters block where it matters. It is a tank problem presenting as an
engine fault.

**Stray current at Port Kembla.** A boat berthed in a working industrial port
with heavy shore power infrastructure sits in an environment where anodes can
disappear for reasons other than galvanic action. Track the wastage rate rather
than assuming a replacement interval.

**Trailer-boat neglect, of a specific kind.** A large part of this fleet is
trailered, which changes the maintenance profile entirely: less immersion, less
growth, less galvanic exposure — but flushing discipline after each use becomes
the thing that matters, and it is the thing most often skipped.

**Layup between uses.** A real winter and a fleet that quietens.

## The trade

Boating infrastructure is compact — Wollongong Harbour and Belmore Basin, Port
Kembla, and Shellharbour's boat harbour. Local marine service exists and is
sized to that fleet.

What the region has in unusual measure is **general heavy engineering**, from
the steelworks and the industries around it: machining, fabrication and diesel
work at a scale most coastal towns cannot match. For a rebuild that means
components do not have to travel.

**Sydney is an hour north.** Use it — for dealer breadth and comparison quotes
on anything substantial. The traffic goes both ways, because Illawarra hardstand
is generally easier to obtain and cheaper than Sydney's.

## Booking

The cooler months, when the fleet is less active and yards have room.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why does my engine only struggle offshore?**
Motion stirs settled water and sludge off the tank bottom into the pickup.
Harbour testing will not reproduce it.

**Why are my anodes going quickly at Port Kembla?**
An industrial shore power environment raises the possibility of stray current.
Investigate rather than simply fitting more.

**Should I get a Sydney quote?**
On anything substantial, yes — it is an hour away and the market is far deeper.

**Is yard time cheaper here than Sydney?**
Generally easier to get and less expensive, which is why some work comes south.

**What matters most for a trailered boat?**
Flushing after each use. It replaces most of what a moored boat needs, and it is
the thing most often skipped.
