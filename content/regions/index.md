# Marine diesel by Australian region

The same engine fails differently depending on where it floats. Warm silty water
kills heat exchangers; cold water and a long layup kill everything else. A
tropical port and a Bass Strait port are not the same maintenance problem.

These pages cover what marine diesel ownership actually involves in each region:
the fleet, the water, what comes through local workshops, when the trade has
capacity, and where to go next.

## How this fits with the rest of the site

Each regional page routes to the specific answers rather than trying to be all of
them:

- **Something is wrong** → the symptom pages
- **Who can fix it here** → the mechanic directory for that region
- **What it should cost** → the cost guides
- **Where to buy an engine here** → the regional buying pages

If you already know which of those you need, go straight there. The regional
pages are for the times you do not.
